- **No endorsement.** A verdict describes what the research found about a tool's fitness for a job-search task. It is not a recommendation to buy, and it does not rank vendors that were not assessed.

## The avoid list

Tools and practices the research found harmful or unreliable for a job search:

- **General LLM assistants as career or salary adviser** (Stage 00: Start-here tools): Peer-reviewed work found LLMs recommend different salary asks to different personas given the same facts (earlier models), and career-advice audits found bias. T0-10 rates advisory use Caution and limits LLMs to drafting and rehearsal: never act on an LLM's number, and check any figure against two data sources. [B · T0-10 L538, L181, L197, L328, L349]
- **Income-share-agreement bootcamps (BloomTech/Lambda School model)** (Stage 03: Skills and technology): Hard disqualifier D1: the CFPB's April 2024 consent order found BloomTech misdescribed its income-share agreements as non-loans and inflated placement claims; its last day of instruction is reported as 31 Dec 2025. [A · T0-10 L544, L95]
- **Exam-dump sites** (Stage 04: Certifications): Dump material for AWS MLA-C01 is sold openly in 2026, and relying on it destroys the only thing a multiple-choice credential signals. No independent cheating-rate measurement exists for AWS, Google or Microsoft exams. [C · T0-11 L128, L382]
- **Read.cv** (Stage 05: Portfolio): Hard disqualifier D4: Perplexity acquired it in January 2025 and users had until 16 May 2025 to export. Keep portfolio work on exportable, own-domain hosting. [A · T0-10 L550, L95, L145]
- **LazyApply** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): Hard disqualifier D2: LinkedIn's terms prohibit the automation it performs. Trustpilot shows about 2.4/5 on roughly 107 reviews, with wrong answers, off-target applications and refund complaints, and T0-04 R6 rejects mass auto-apply. [C · T0-10 L570, L259; T0-04 L130]
- **Simplify Autopilot** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): Per a competitor's reading of Simplify's help centre it has no review step, and wrong screening answers are the most frequent failure in auto-apply complaints. T0-10 rates it Caution (beta, no outcome evidence) and T0-04 R6 rejects auto-apply; T0-10 re-audits it if it leaves beta. [D · T0-10 L559, L260, L161, L185; T0-04 L130]
- **Jobscan Auto Apply** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): Reported but not re-verified, with no outcome evidence; T0-10 rates it Caution and T0-04 R6 rejects auto-apply. Jobscan's scanner has its own record. [D · T0-10 L563, L71; T0-04 L130]
- **Hosted cloud auto-apply services (FastApply, LoopCV, JobCopilot, Massive, AIApply)** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): Not individually audited; one vendor's own test of eight bots claims only three actually click Submit. Cloud agents likely submit from data-centre IPs, which Greenhouse treats as a high-risk fraud signal (T0-10's inference), and T0-04 R6 rejects mass auto-apply. [D · T0-10 L572, L261, L68; T0-04 L130]
- **Sonara (relaunched under BOLD)** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): The original service shut down on 1 Feb 2024, and the brand was reportedly relaunched under BOLD, per competitor sources. T0-10 rates it Caution and T0-04 R6 rejects auto-apply. [D · T0-10 L571, L262; T0-04 L130]
- **Scoutify (alerts and 'Sniper' auto-apply)** (Stage 06: Job search and applications): The only evidence is the vendor's own blog (T0-10 Caution; about $5, $15 or $25 a week, vendor-stated), and T0-10 relied on that blog for other tools. Its auto-apply mode falls under T0-04 R6, which rejects auto-apply. [D · T0-10 L557; T0-04 L130]
- **Third-party LinkedIn automation (PhantomBuster and similar bots and scraping extensions)** (Stage 07: Networking and visibility): Hard disqualifier D2: LinkedIn prohibits bots and extensions that automate activity and names account restriction as the consequence. T0-05 rates PhantomBuster Avoid: at 30 or fewer contacts a week, automation adds restriction risk with no evidence of benefit. [A · T0-10 L575, L157; T0-05 L339, L379]
- **RocketReach** (Stage 07: Networking and visibility): The lowest find rate (29.8%) in Pin's vendor-run audit of 1.3M lookups, against vendor claims of 90–99%; paid plans start at $329 a year (competitor-reported). T0-05's verdict: skip. [C · T0-05 L337, L64]
- **Clay** (Stage 07: Networking and visibility): Built for go-to-market teams, with credit-based pricing (Launch $185 a month on annual billing); T0-05's verdict at 30 or fewer sends a week is skip. [C · T0-05 L338]
- **Paid email finders (Wiza, ContactOut, Forager)** (Stage 07: Networking and visibility): Find rates of 55.2%, 48.2% and 45.9% in Pin's vendor-run audit, against vendor claims of 90–99%. T0-05 finds paid tooling mostly unnecessary at 30 or fewer sends a week, where free tiers plus manual verification are enough. [C · T0-05 L64, L476]
- **Final Round AI (live Interview Copilot)** (Stage 08: Interviewing): Hard disqualifier D3: undisclosed live assistance in an assessed setting. Amazon reportedly tells recruiters to disqualify candidates found using AI tools; follow each employer's stated AI policy. [B · T0-10 L588, L297, L174]
- **Overlay interview copilots (Cluely, Interview Coder, Parakeet AI, LeetCode Wizard)** (Stage 08: Interviewing): Hard disqualifier D3: undisclosed live assistance in an assessed setting breaks the stated policies of employers that prohibit AI in interviews and risks disqualification. Disclosed use where an employer explicitly permits it is a different case. [B · T0-10 L589, L297, L302, L200]
- **PayPal (for freelance invoices)** (Stage 11: Tracks and transitions): About 8–9% payment friction for India-based freelancers, per a payments vendor; T0-18 advises against it for large invoices. [C · T0-18 L1249, L1424]
- **Fiverr** (Stage 11: Tracks and transitions): It lists AI strategy consultants at $10–125 an hour and AI agent builds at about $295 a project; T0-18 judges it not suitable for senior work. [C · T0-18 L1233, L1419]
- **Freelancers Union Contract Creator** (Stage 11: Tracks and transitions): No IP clause is mentioned, so it is too thin for AI work: in India a contractor is the first owner of the code, and an assignment silent on duration and territory defaults to five years and India only. ## Evidence and limits

- Built from the tool catalogs, findings and operating rules of the October 2026 research sessions. The main source is T0-10, the 12-stage tooling audit (retrieved 2026-10-21); T0-04, T0-05, T0-11, T0-13, T0-14, T0-02, T0-17, T0-07, T0-18, T0-06, T0-09, T0-08 and T1-03 add the tools of their domains. No tool was tested hands-on: verdicts rest on vendor documentation, pricing pages, reviews, datasets and studies as each session graded them, so no verdict validates outcomes. Verdicts follow the sessions. T0-10 Recommended maps to use, Viable to use-with-care and Avoid to avoid. T0-10 Caution maps to use-with-care when the session names a safe use or control, to watch when the blocker is newness or unverified status, and to avoid where the sessions' operating rules say not to rely on it: products whose core function is auto-applying (rejected by T0-04 R6 and T0-10.F4) and LLM career or salary advice. Explicit 'skip' or 'avoid' verdicts in T0-05 and T0-18 also map to avoid. Where sessions conflict, the record says so and takes the more cautious verdict. 'evidence' is the weakest grade among the claims in 'why', on the cited session's own A-D scale (A primary or measured, B transparent secondary, C vendor, third-party or single-source, D recall or judgement); C and D claims are attributed in the text. 'as_of' is the earliest retrieval date among the cited sources; re-verify statuses and prices within 90 days of it. Out of scope by design: communities, including Blind (career/07-networking/communities-guide.md); credentials themselves, including T0-10's certification rows, and engineering frameworks (both in data/skills_ontology.json); and employer-side systems that candidates do not choose (ATSs, AI screening layers, engineering-analytics tools). Beyz and Interviews by AI are omitted because T0-10 found no evidence about them (grade E). Records apply research/ERRATA.md U13, U16 and U17. Tax, visa and legal entries are pointers, not advice.
