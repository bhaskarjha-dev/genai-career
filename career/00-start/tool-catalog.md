## Data hygiene and security policy (T0-10)

Before uploading a résumé, connecting an account or installing a browser extension, classify the data you are sharing [P · T0-10 L165-L171, L343]:

| Data tier | Examples | Where it may go |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Résumé stripped of home address, date of birth and ID numbers | Acceptable for reputable external career tools [P · T0-10 L166] |
| **Tier 2** | Offer letters, compensation history and equity documents | Only tools whose retention and training terms you have read, or redact company names and personal fields first [P · T0-10 L167, L343] |
| **Tier 3** | Government ID or liveness selfie | Employer-initiated verification only, via a link reached through a known channel [A/B · T0-10 L168] |
| **Tier 4** | Interview audio or screen capture, and any LinkedIn credentials or session cookies | **Do not give** to third-party tools, live overlays or scraping extensions [P · T0-10 L169] |

- **Compartmentalisation and terms:** use a dedicated email and phone number for job-search tooling (for compartmentalisation, not repeat-application evasion), check retention periods, training clauses (for example Kickresume's anonymised-document clause), subprocessors and export/delete paths, and remove unused browser extensions [P/C · T0-10 L163, L170-L171, L339, L343].
- **Documented threat cases (T0-10):**
  - **LayerX "AiFrame" extension campaign (Feb 2026):** researchers documented 30+ fake "AI assistant" Chrome extensions totalling roughly 260,000-300,000 installs that loaded remote content so behaviour could change without a store update [B · T0-10 L95, L338]. Treat extension permissions like credentials.
  - **McHire / Paradox.ai breach (disclosed Jul 2025):** a test admin account with a trivial default password plus an IDOR flaw exposed up to 64 million applicant records on an employer recruiting chatbot (Paradox stated five records were accessed, by the researchers, and fixed it) [B · T0-10 L80, L334]. Keep voluntary answers in employer chat widgets minimal.

## The avoid list

Tools and practices the research found harmful or unreliable for a job search:

- **General LLM assistants as career or salary adviser** (Stage 00: Start-here tools): Peer-reviewed studies (PLOS ONE 2025), a 17-chatbot bias audit and NeurIPS gender-wage audit (T0-10), plus Oct 2026 preprints on 2026-era models (COI: Claude included) confirm persona-dependent career and salary-advice bias persists. Anchor career and pay choices on independent benchmarks instead. [B · T0-10 L349, L538; T0-26 L63, L376, L442]
- **ChatGPT Atlas** (Stage 00: Start-here tools): Discontinued on 2026-08-09 and folded into ChatGPT (live-verified 2026-11-19); researchers also demonstrated cross-site prompt-injection data extraction against agentic browsers. [B · T0-26 L60, L165, L378]
- **General browser agents for job applying (Comet, Claude in Chrome, Gemini in Chrome)** (Stage 00: Start-here tools): No job board checked sanctions browser agents for applying, agent blocking rates are rising per HUMAN Security May 2026 data, and cross-site prompt-injection risks apply (COI noted for Claude in Chrome). [C · T0-26 L60, L165, L377]
- **Income-share-agreement bootcamps (BloomTech/Lambda School model)** (Stage 03: Skills and technology): Hard disqualifier: CFPB consent order (2024-04-17) found BloomTech misrepresented ISAs as non-loans and inflated placement claims; California BPPE confirms final instruction 31 Dec 2025. Treat ISA, pay-after-placement and EMI schemes as loans; no Indian ISA provider has audited outcomes. [B · T0-10 L544; T0-68 L72, L398]
- **Vendor-awarded 'Certified AI Consultant / Fractional CAIO' badges (e.g. chiefaiofficer.com)** (Stage 03: Skills and technology): Fails T0-68 gate G4: vendor-awarded title with undisclosed pricing and zero independent assessment or employer recognition. [C · T0-68 L83, L137, L302]
- **Unverified private AI certification bodies (ARTiBA, GSDC, Blockchain Council, IABAC, USAII)** (Stage 04: Certifications): Zero verified accreditation (GSDC cites ISO/IEC 17024 PCB-168 without naming the accreditation body), 0/29 US+India posting hits, and exam-dump listings found for USAII. - **Read.cv** (Stage 05: Portfolio): Hard disqualifier D4: acquired by Perplexity on 2025-01-17 and wound down with export closing on 2025-05-16. Keep portfolio and writing canonical on exportable, own-domain hosting. [A · T0-20 L207, L328; T0-10 L550, L95]
