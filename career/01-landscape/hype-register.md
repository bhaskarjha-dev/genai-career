# GenAI Career Hype Register: Circulating Claims Checked Against Evidence

> Decision D-012 Hype Register: 19 boom and doom claims about GenAI careers that the research sessions traced, tested or corrected, each with its origin, the measured evidence and a decision rule.

**Purpose and governance.** Implemented under [Decision D-012](../../research/DECISIONS.md) and EGP-1.0 §10.9 [A · T1-02 L720–L783]. Under the repository's evidence standard ([CONTRIBUTING.md §3](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#3-writing-standard-egp-10-in-brief)), a statistic whose origin cannot be traced is Grade **E** and is never asserted in career guidance; a statistic that is real but quoted outside its scope is corrected and shown beside the common version. Both kinds live here, with how each claim arose, so that readers can see why the guides do not repeat them.

**Where each claim comes from.** Every row says how the claim reached this register:

- **Traced:** a research session followed the claim to a named report, survey, article or vendor page.
- **No origin found:** a session searched for the origin and found none, or found a trail that ends in no measurement.
- **Tested:** a session tested the claim against data without tracing where it started.
- **Repository record:** the claim sat in this repository's own research brief or Phase 0 sessions, and a later session corrected it (see [ERRATA](../../research/ERRATA.md)). These rows record our own corrections; they say nothing about how far the statement circulates elsewhere.

Quotation marks show wording that a session quotes; claims without them are paraphrased. Decision rules are this repository's judgement (Grade D) and point to the page that owns the detail.

## Claim-archaeology rules (T1-02 H0–H9)

Entries follow the protocol in T1-02 §10.9 [A · T1-02 L726–L737]:

- **Symmetry (H9):** boom claims and doom claims get the same trace. Of the 19 entries, 11 overstate the upside (boom), 5 overstate the downside (doom) and 3 are mixed (a boom claim and a doom claim together, or no clear direction).
- **Origin tracing (H1–H4):** find the originating report, survey, filing or speech, then check its population, denominator, unit and conflicts of interest (`COI`).
- **Four outcomes (H5):** **O1** method found and adequate, scope corrected; **O2** method unavailable, so Grade **E (origin-untraceable)**, register only; **O3** method found but defective, so the corrected reading is published with the defect named; **O4** inflated downstream, so the original statement is shown beside the common version.
- **Phrase rules:** "widely reported" and "many sources say" are not accepted as support [T1-02 L441]; "widely cited" is allowed only with a count of outlets and dates (H8) [T1-02 L736].
- **Expiry and recheck (H7):** entries are rechecked quarterly or when a primary method is published, and expire after 12 months unless renewed [T1-02 L735].

## Evidence and limits

- **Scope.** The register holds claims that a research session traced, tested, or found in this repository's own earlier records. It measures no claim's reach: "circulating" here means that a session met the claim in a source it names. The absence of a claim does not endorse it.
- **Grades.** Each row keeps the grades its sessions gave; vendor, employer and platform-owner figures carry COI. Decision rules are judgement (D).
- **Re-verification.** HR-018 states a V0 status, so this page is due on 2026-12-03, 14 days after the owner page's check; HR-004 quotes monthly series whose live figures sit in the [market overview](market-overview.md). Entries are rechecked quarterly and expire after 12 months unless renewed [T1-02 L735].
- **Process.** To add or challenge an entry, follow the H0–H9 protocol and the CAP-1 conflict rubric in [CONTRIBUTING.md §3](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#3-writing-standard-egp-10-in-brief).
