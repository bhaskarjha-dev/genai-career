## What the role actually looks like

Across the 33 postings sampled in November 2026 (14 with enough requirement text to code skills, ±26 percentage points), agentic engineering focuses on building and operating multi-step, tool-using LLM systems together with their surrounding production harness: **orchestration and multi-agent workflows (8/14), tool calling and gateways (8/14), evaluation harnesses (7/14), reliability and operational ownership (5/14), and fluency with AI-assisted development tools (5/14)** [M · T0-57 L61, L98, L162–L184]. By contrast, Phase 0's textbook vocabulary — durable execution (1/14, Amazon Step Functions), containment/sandboxing (1/14, Ai2), and human-in-the-loop approvals (0/14 in the coded set, seen in Wipro outside it) — appears less frequently verbatim, though Check Point's Black Hat USA 2026 disclosure of 11 vulnerabilities across major agent frameworks (unauthorised tool invocation, remote code execution; Grade C) reinforces that containment remains load-bearing production work [M/C · T0-57 L61, L177–L180, L344].

The umbrella spans **five distinct day-to-day archetypes** [B/M · T0-57 L65, L100]:
1. **Agent platform and harness builders** (Ai2, OpenAI API Agents, ServiceNow Agentic AI Harness & Quality): Build shared agent runtimes, tool gateways, memory services, trace infrastructure, and evaluation harnesses that compound across products [A/B · T0-57 L98, L270].
2. **Product agent engineers** (Salesforce Agentforce, Amazon AWS Transform / AgentCore, Microsoft Sales Platform, CUBE, APAS, Juro): Build domain-specific multi-agent product features, memory/context state, and Python/FastAPI or full-stack services [B · T0-57 L100, L508–L514].
3. **Forward-deployed / customer-facing agent builders** (ServiceNow Moveworks, Sierra, Simple AI): Embed with enterprise clients to turn an agent platform into production customer deployments [B · T0-57 L65, L306].
4. **Enterprise and IT-services delivery** (Capgemini, Wipro *Agentic Hive*, EPAM, Opsfleet): Deliver client multi-agent workflows across hyperscaler stacks (Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, Bedrock) and open-source frameworks [B · T0-57 L100, L320].
5. **Software-delivery-lifecycle (SDLC) agent builders** (Workday): Build internal or developer-facing coding and automation agents [C · T0-57 L100, L259].

**Framework reality and status (live-verified 2026-11-19).** Named open-source frameworks appear less often than hype suggests: **LangGraph appeared in 4/14** and **LangChain in 3/14** coded postings, while Big Tech postings prioritise proprietary stacks (Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft 365 Agents SDK) alongside MCP (3/14: Amazon, Microsoft, SAP; SAP also names A2A) [B/M · T0-57 L170–L176, L309]. Microsoft's **Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0 reached general availability on 2–3 April 2026**, unifying Semantic Kernel and AutoGen, and **AutoGen moved into community-managed maintenance mode** (confirmed on microsoft.com and github.com on 2026-11-19) [B · T0-57 L62, L341]. Even so, two in-window postings (Microsoft 2026-09-11; Capgemini Pune 2026-08-19) still listed AutoGen among acceptable stacks, and Capgemini listed CrewAI — treat AutoGen and CrewAI as legacy/optional options rather than headline skills [B/M · T0-57 L62, L342–L343].

**Mid-level versus senior and staff scope.** The role skews heavily senior: **22 of 33 sampled postings** are senior or above by title or years, with a stated minimum experience median of **5 years (range 3–8+, n = 9)** [M · T0-57 L58, L156]. Senior and Staff roles own SLOs, on-call/game-day reliability (Amazon), multi-node orchestration engines (Salesforce), and reviewing AI-generated code with production rigour (Microsoft) [B · T0-57 L68, L509–L514].

## Career path, India demand and market signals

## Interview themes and preparation notes

Agent-focused hiring is at the leading edge of **AI-native interview redesign**, creating sharp divergence between employers [A/C · T0-57 L64, L218–L227]:

- **Sierra's AI-native onsite (primary employer blog, Grade A; live-verified 2026-11-19):** Launched in April 2026 for `Software Engineer, Agent`, Sierra replaced traditional coding/algorithm screens with a **system-design screen** plus a three-part **Plan → Build (2 hours) → Review** onsite (with a debugging round in pilot). Candidates use **any AI coding tools and frameworks they choose** during Build and are evaluated in Review on product judgement, code quality, path to production, failure handling, and **how they directed AI tools** [A · T0-57 L64, L220, L600].
- **Conventional and restricted loops elsewhere:** Contrast Sierra with Anthropic (which links candidate AI-usage restrictions prohibiting AI in live rounds; B, COI) and ServiceNow (~4 rounds over 3–5 weeks covering live coding or agent-workflow design such as arresting a runaway tool-call loop, and behavioural; C) [B/C · T0-57 L223, L225]. Aggregated candidate reports (127 reports on Dataford, Grade C) rank **prompting, tool calling/function calling, and RAG** first, followed by orchestration frameworks and security boundaries [C · T0-57 L64, L224, L227].
- **Automated screening notice:** Ai2 explicitly discloses using Greenhouse Talent Matching and LinkedIn Hiring Assistant to match applications against requirements (with an opt-out link), rewarding clear keyword alignment with real work [A · T0-57 L291, L400].
- **Proof of work and red flags:** Employers explicitly name **production agents with sandboxing and cost/latency tuning** (Ai2), **evaluation harnesses for nondeterministic software** (Ai2, Amazon, Microsoft), **on-call and game-day reliability** (Amazon), **rigorous review of agent-generated code** (Microsoft), and **"production systems, not prototypes"** (ServiceNow) [A/B · T0-57 L68, L231]. Note that Phase 0's specific trace-replay and failure-injection wording remains practice-curated (`[P]`) rather than verbatim in employer JDs [P · T0-57 L233, L363]. Avoid framework lists without production outcomes, demo-only prototypes, or low-code workflow tools presented as agentic engineering [P · T0-57 L293]. Practise with the [application prep pack](../../08-interviewing/prep-packs.md#application-pack) (no model answers).
