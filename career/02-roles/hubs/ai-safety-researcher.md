## What the role actually looks like

Frontier AI Safety / Alignment Researcher is empirical safety research across alignment, control, mechanistic interpretability, scheming science and dangerous-capability evaluations [A/B · T0-60 L76, L368, L412]. Across T0-60's extended November 2026 sample (n = 23 postings across 8 organisations), work clusters into three employer-driven variants [A/B · T0-60 L302, L368]:

- **Frontier-lab alignment, control and frontier assurance (12/23: Anthropic ×5, OpenAI ×5, Google DeepMind ×2):** alignment science, safeguards evaluation methods, Frontier Red Team dangerous-capability evals, agent oversight, recursive self-improvement risk measurement and misalignment research — notably, OpenAI's Misalignment Research role asks for 4+ years in AI red-teaming, security research or adversarial ML, creating a direct security-to-safety bridge [A/B · T0-60 L302, L368, L617–L623, L632–L636].
- **Government AI Security Institutes (4/23: UK AISI ×4):** Alignment Red Team, Control Red Team, Model Transparency and Sub-Team Lead roles — empirical safety research rather than operational enterprise security [B · T0-60 L108, L302, L624–L627].
- **Independent and non-profit research organisations (7/23: Goodfire ×3, METR ×2, Apollo Research, FAR.AI):** mechanistic interpretability (4/23 = 17% overall; 4/15 = 27% base across Goodfire ×3 and UK AISI Model Transparency), science of scheming (Apollo), evaluation execution and standards (METR) and pod-based alignment research (FAR.AI) [B/M · T0-60 L302, L316, L368, L628–L631, L637–L639].

**Skills reality (base n = 15; lower bounds from mostly excerpted text; ±25 pp Wilson intervals — cross-check only).** **Empirical research experience (8/15, 53%)** and alignment methods (8/15, 53%) are the most frequent explicit requirements, followed by safety evaluations (5/15, 33%), ML fundamentals including SFT/RLHF/RL (4/15, 27%), interpretability (4/15, 27% — Must for interpretability teams), red teaming (4/15, 27%), Python (3/15, 20% — explicitly stated for Anthropic Alignment interviews and Fellows), novel research/publications (3/15, 20%), fellowship or independent research evidence (2/15 plus fellowship postings) and the Inspect evaluation framework (1/15, UK AISI; also referenced by Apollo outside the sample) [M · T0-60 L320–L333, L680–L691]. Explicit PyTorch appeared in only 1 of 15 excerpts (UK AISI: PyTorch or Inspect), reflecting excerpt truncation rather than irrelevance [M · T0-60 L325, L421].

**Proof and credentials.** Zero of 15 base postings name any certification — Anthropic's Alignment posting states explicitly that candidates need not hold formal certifications or education credentials [A/M · T0-60 L79, L373, L617]. Instead, applications ask for a link to the candidate's most relevant empirical work alongside Google Scholar and GitHub URLs (Anthropic), a significant frontier-LLM research project (UK AISI), published alignment or post-training work (Goodfire) or a public research output (Anthropic Fellows) [A/B · T0-60 L372, L424].
