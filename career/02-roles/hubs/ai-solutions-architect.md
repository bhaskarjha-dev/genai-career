## What the role actually looks like

November 2026 postings (n = 23) show that two distinct jobs share the Solutions Architect label: **pre-sales / field architecture** (15 of 23 sampled postings, 65.2%), which sells and de-risks a vendor's models or platform through technical discovery, demos and customer evaluations, and **delivery / customer-success / consulting architecture** (8 of 23, 34.8%), which designs and governs client deployments post-sale or inside a systems integrator [M · T0-62 L68, L110].

- **How responsibilities divide by employer type:** at OpenAI, an Applied AI Architect owns the customer's technical path from pre-sales discovery through production adoption while separate delivery teams implement; at Anthropic, Applied AI Architects partner with account executives on enterprise/startup evaluations (including $10M+ evaluations on the manager track); at Microsoft, Cloud Solution Architects sit inside the **post-sales customer-success unit**, not pre-sales; at Big Four and IT-services consultancies (Accenture, Capgemini, EY, PwC, Cognizant), architects design end-to-end client architectures spanning model routing, identity, residency, cost and Responsible AI [A/B · T0-62 L68, L114–L121, L184, L228].
- **What postings actually gate on vs the scorecard above (n = 23 extracts; floors, roughly ±20 pp):**
  - **Customer-facing technical experience (95.7%, 22/23) and pre-sales (60.9%, 14/23)** are the decisive hiring gates — both are missing from or under-tiered on the scorecard above [M · T0-62 L72, L146–L147, L438].
  - **Generic cloud (52.2%, 12/23; Axial platform cross-check 62%, n = 16,927) holds as Must**, whereas AWS specifically appeared in only 26.1% (6/23, matching Azure 30.4% and GCP 30.4%) — meaning multi-cloud or target-vendor cloud matters rather than AWS exclusively [M/B · T0-62 L148–L152, L162, L438].
  - **RAG (26.1%, 6/23) and evaluations (17.4%, 4/23: Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Tribe AI)** appear prominently in vendor postings and interviews despite sitting outside the scorecard's headline skills [M · T0-62 L153–L155]. `agents` appeared in 56.5% (13/23), held at Important because targeted "Agentic AI Architect" queries inflated the share [M · T0-62 L149, L505]. Axial's US AI-architecture index (n = 16,927, 2026-08-25) also surfaces Python (44%), observability (39%) and foundation models (38%) [B · T0-62 L162].
- **Proof of work:** Anthropic's opened Startups posting asks for production LLM apps, context engineering, evaluations and a track record of selling technical products [A, COI · T0-62 L203, L600]. Reference architectures (Accenture) and cost/latency constraints (PwC) appear in consultancy postings, whereas **infrastructure-as-code appeared in 0 of 23 extracts** — pair [P11](../../05-portfolio/project-archetypes.md#p11) with an eval-backed demo and a 10-minute customer-scenario walkthrough [A/B/P · T0-62 L203, L442, L866].
