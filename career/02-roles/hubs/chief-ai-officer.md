## What the role actually looks like

A Chief AI Officer (CAIO) is an enterprise or public-sector executive accountable for AI strategy, adoption and governance—**not** a standard open-market engineering vacancy [A/B · T0-65 L43, L67, L283]. Across the November 2026 dossier's 15 CAIO records (and October 2026 appointment roundups), five distinct institutional archetypes emerge [B/C · T0-65 L283–288]:

| Archetype | Reporting line & mandate | Representative examples in sample | Practical reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US federal / state principal officer** | Agency head or Deputy Secretary; policy-making, governance boards and lifecycle risk compliance | US federal agencies under OMB M-25-21 [A]; Illinois DoIT CAIO (reports to Deputy Secretary [B]); State of Arkansas CAIO [B] | OMB M-25-21 (issued 2025-04-03) explicitly permits designating an existing CIO, CDO or CTO—creating designated officials, not an open hiring wave [A · T0-65 L65, L526] |
| **Higher-education and healthcare officer** | CIO, COO office or health-system leadership; ethical adoption and clinical/institutional AI governance | UCSF Health (VP/CHAIO [B]); College of Charleston (*Director, Chief AI Officer*, reports to CIO [B]); UNC System (in COO office [B]); UCLA Health [C] | Often director- or VP-grade rather than true C-suite; US on-site [B/C · T0-65 L285, L355, L357, L561, L565, L572, L574] |
| **Operational bank executive (India public sector)** | Fixed-term General Manager leading enterprise ML/AI/NLP platforms at 100,000+ user scale, governance and independent model validation | State Bank of India *GM & Chief AI Officer* (`CRPD/SCO/2026-27/30`, 2026-10-19) [B · T0-65 L76, L286, L560] | Demanding operational + regulatory remit (RBI directions, DPDP Act); 3-year contract renewable to 5; IC years excluded [B · T0-65 L76, L292, L560] |
| **Dual-hatted / internally promoted corporate CAIO** | Existing CDO, CISO, group chief of staff, SVP or co-founder adding the CAIO remit | October 2026 roundup: CPSC (stays CDO), Barclays (group chief of staff), Lydonia (CDO & CISO), ModMed (co-founder) [C · T0-65 L67, L288, L352] | At least 4 of 10 announced October 2026 appointments were internal moves or dual-hatted [C · T0-65 L67, L696] |
| **Private-sector retained-search corporate CAIO** | Reports to CEO or CTO; leads 10–50+ AI/ML engineers, vendor/build-vs-buy choices, governance and board/investor reporting | Axial Search evergreen US CAIO template (role closed; talent-network profile) [C, COI · T0-65 L287, L563] | Rarely posted publicly; filled via retained search or internal promotion [B/C · T0-65 L67, L88] |

**What text-visible CAIO postings actually require (n = 7, lower bounds).** Strategy, vision or adoption leadership appears in **7 of 7 (100%)**, general responsible-AI governance/policy/compliance in **6 of 7 (86%)**, and technical platform/delivery depth in **3 of 7 (43%**: SBI, Axial, Riverside) [M · T0-65 L309–315, L609–612]. Remarkably, **0 of 7** text-visible postings named AI-specific regulatory frameworks (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001)—Axial named GDPR, HIPAA and SOC 2, and SBI referenced RBI directions and India's DPDP Act [B/M · T0-65 L292, L316, L611]. Only 2 of 7 text-visible records stated CEO reporting (recruiter listing; Axial CEO or CTO), against CIO (1) and Deputy Secretary (1) [M · T0-65 L321, L617].

**Decision rule.** Do **not** build a job search around public CAIO job boards—only **2 dated in-window employer postings** surfaced globally across the November 2026 search [M · T0-65 L67, L88, L699]. Treat CAIO as a seat reached through (1) internal promotion from CDO, CTO, CISO or VP of AI, (2) public-sector, bank or higher-education tenders, or (3) retained executive search [B/C · T0-65 L88, L352].

Live web verification on 2026-11-19 further highlights a structural authority gap reported by IBM's own Institute for Business Value CAIO study (secondary press coverage, Grade C): while CAIOs carry broad strategy mandates, only **18%** report full control over direct AI investment budgets.

**Federal mandate reality (OMB M-25-21).** Issued **2025-04-03** (superseding M-24-10, confirmed in force and live-verified 2026-11-19), OMB Memorandum M-25-21 required each US federal agency (excluding national security systems) to retain or designate a CAIO within 60 days—at SES/SL/ST level in CFO Act agencies and GS-14+ elsewhere—and convened a CAIO Council that sunsets five years after issuance unless OMB extends it [A · T0-65 L65, L526]. Because existing CIOs, CDOs or CTOs may be designated (as at the CPSC), M-25-21 creates statutory accountability rather than mass external hiring [A/C · T0-65 L65].

**Title confusion and posting traps:**
- **Not every "CAIO" is C-suite.** College of Charleston's *Director, Chief AI Officer* ($150K–$175K) reports to the CIO [B · T0-65 L285, L357, L565], and ZipRecruiter's "1,000+ Chief AI Officer" headline count mixes fractional, advisory and loosely matched titles [B · T0-65 L701].
- **Evergreen search-firm pages are not open vacancies.** Axial Search's US CAIO page states the role is closed and collects candidates for future roles [C, COI · T0-65 L438, L563]. Meanwhile, an AI-governance job board showed just **1 open CAIO role** (UCSF Health CHAIO) in early November 2026 [B · T0-65 L67, L305, L697].
- **Distinct from VP/Head of AI and Fractional CAIO.** See [VP / Head of AI](vp-head-of-ai.md) for functional engineering/transformation leads and [Fractional Head of AI / Fractional CAIO](fractional-ai-leader.md) for part-time, multi-client executive seats.
- **Aliases observed:** *Chief Data/AI Officer*, *Chief AI & Innovation Officer*, *Chief Health AI Officer (CHAIO)*, *General Manager & Chief AI Officer*, *Director, Chief AI Officer*, *Chief Technology & AI Officer* [B/C · T0-65 L288, L290, L560–574].

## Career path, India demand and market signals

**Entry routes and prerequisites.** Where stated, postings ask for **12+ years** with 5+ at director/VP/C-level (Axial [C, COI]) or **15+ years** in banking/BFSI/IT with 3+ in senior AI leadership excluding individual-contributor years (SBI [B]) [M · T0-65 L323, L702]. Most real-world corporate appointments are internal promotions or lateral executive expansions from CDO, CISO, chief of staff, SVP or co-founder seats [C · T0-65 L67, L352]. Findem reports over 60% of CAIOs have 0–2 years of tenure at their current employer (company tenure, not time in role) [C · T0-65 L352].

**Credentials and executive programmes.** Only **1 of 7** text-visible CAIO postings mentions a certification (SBI lists any ML/AI/NLP certification as *preferred only*, while requiring a technical degree such as B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech/M.Sc/M.Stat/MCA) [B/M · T0-65 L323, L560, L703]. Executive programmes (e.g., LBS £22,000, Imperial £9,000) and IAPP AIGP ($649–$799) are marketplace figures reported by third-party guides, not hiring gates [C · T0-65 L351, L517].

**Durability and exit uncertainty.** Average Fortune 500 CAIO tenure is reported at **~2.2 years** (Avasant [C]), and widespread dual-hatting (CDO/CISO/CIO holding the CAIO designation) supports viewing standalone CAIO seats as potentially transitional—even as IBM's CEO survey (COI) claims respondents expect CAIO influence to grow by 2030 [C · T0-65 L89, L353, L694]. No sourced exit-path analysis exists [E · T0-65 L353, L411, L735].

**India demand and the SBI benchmark.** Public-sector and regulated financial institutions anchor visible India CAIO hiring. State Bank of India's recruitment notice **`CRPD/SCO/2026-27/30`** (dated 2026-10-19, applications to 2026-11-09; read in full as a hosted PDF copy [B] and live-verified against `sbi.bank.in` and press reporting on 2026-11-19) advertised one fixed-term *General Manager & Chief AI Officer* post alongside five DGM/architect AI posts (Risk/Operational Analytics, AI Governance, Data Engineering/AIOps, Enterprise/AI Solution Architects): age 40–55, technical degree required, 15+ years with 3+ years senior AI leadership (IC years excluded), 3-year term renewable by 2 years (max 5), Mumbai or anywhere in India [B · T0-65 L76, L358, L560]. Separately, IBM India's 2025-10-06 survey release reported 67% of surveyed Indian enterprises *expecting* to appoint a CAIO within two years—an expectation survey, not realised headcount [B, COI · T0-65 L358, L700]. Adjacent India governance titles include VP – AI Governance (e.g., Nomura Mumbai [B · T0-65 L573]); see [Governance careers](../../11-tracks/governance-careers.md).

**Pay signals (pointer to owner).** Salaried pay tables live in [pay benchmarks](../../09-compensation/pay-benchmarks.md) (decision D-019); fractional rates live in [freelance and fractional practice](../../11-tracks/freelance-fractional.md). As T0-65 decision anchors (as of November 2026), CAIO pay splits sharply across two markets [B/C · T0-65 L68, L327–337]:
- **Public sector, higher education and India bank fixed-term:** US posted base bands **$117,734–$200,000** (Arkansas $117.7K–$192.1K, College of Charleston $150K–$175K, Illinois DoIT $160K–$200K [B · T0-65 L330–332, L643–645]); SBI GM & CAIO negotiable CTC ceiling **up to ₹160 lakh (₹1.60 Cr)** (90% fixed, 10% variable, annual increment up to 5% [B · T0-65 L329, L642]).

## Interview themes and preparation notes

Corporate CAIO selection rarely resembles a standard technical loop and is largely undocumented in public sources [E · T0-65 L400, L724]. Two documented selection mechanisms appear in T0-65:

| Employer / channel | Documented selection stages | Duration & structure | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| **State Bank of India** (public-sector bank, India) | Online application → experience shortlisting → **100-mark interview** including a **10-minute presentation** (GM/DGM posts) → merit list on interview marks → one-at-a-time CTC negotiation | No written exam; qualifying marks set by bank | B · T0-65 L343, L709 |
| **Retained executive search** (US/PE-backed corporate) | Search brief settled → executive recruiter outreach & assessment → shortlist of 3–5 candidates → executive/board interviews (later stages undisclosed) | 4–5 weeks to shortlist (HMN Capital); 10–16 weeks PE-backed or 4–6 months end-to-end (KORE1) | C, COI · T0-65 L344, L704, L710 |

**Preparation notes (curated evidence, no model answers per D-018):**
- **Proof of work that differentiates:** Documented AI delivery at enterprise scale (SBI specifies 100,000+ users, MLOps/GenAI deployment security and oversight of independent model validation), governance cadence and AI inventory, vendor and build-vs-buy decisions, and board-level risk/ROI reporting [B/C/P · T0-65 L350, L356, L716].
- **Positioning rule:** Do not apply the "Chief AI Officer" label to a non-executive advisory or IC seat; title inflation is a red flag in executive search [P · T0-65 L717].
- **Clarify authority before accepting:** Probe direct vs dotted-line reporting to the CEO, actual control over AI capital and headcount budgets, and whether the seat is dual-hatted or funded with a multi-year charter [C/P · T0-65 L89, L357].
