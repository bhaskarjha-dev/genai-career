## What the role actually looks like

A Fractional Head of AI or Fractional Chief AI Officer serves one or more organisations part-time—typically 1–5 days a week or 8–32 hours a month—owning AI strategy, governance, executive communication, organisational enablement and use-case ROI without holding a full-time W-2 executive seat [B/C · T0-66 L151, L168, L530–539]. Crucially, T0-66 updates the legacy assumption that fractional leaders work exclusively on monthly retainers (K1 updated): across the 13 qualifying postings retrieved on 2026-11-13/14, engagements take **four commercial shapes** [B · T0-66 L168, L339]:

- **Month-to-month marketplace agreements** (Go Fractional, handling contracts, invoicing and payouts [B/C · T0-66 L168; live-verified 2026-11-19]);
- **Hourly 1099 contracts** (e.g., Servant's *Fractional AI Delivery Engine Leader*; Estrin VIP's law-firm Fractional CAIO at 10–20 hours/week [B · T0-66 L168, L535–536]);
- **Project-based advisory engagements** (Arootah advisor network; Fractionus project/contract listings [B · T0-66 L168, L529, L534]);
- **Multi-company portfolio days** (1–5 days/week across PE-backed companies or 2–6 days/month for agency/martech clients in the UK [B · T0-66 L168, L530–531]).

**What fractional postings ask for (n = 13, ±27 pp sampling tolerance).** Strategy appears in **11 of 13 (85%)**, governance/risk, executive communication and change/training enablement in **10 of 13 (77% each)**, and use-case ROI, team leadership and prior executive seat (Head, VP, CTO or fractional C-suite) in **9 of 13 (69% each)** [M · T0-66 L54, L121–131, L582–588]. Hands-on LLM depth appears in **6 of 13 (46%)**, RAG/agents and vertical domain expertise (legal, PE/financial services, marketing agency, martech) in **5 of 13 (38% each)**, vendor selection in 3 of 13 (23%), and data strategy or MLOps/LLMOps in 2 of 13 (15%) [M · T0-66 L132–136, L589–594]. Excluding the four shared-template Axial Search pages (n = 9) keeps the same core shape: strategy 78%, governance/comms/change/hands-on LLM 67% each, ROI/team/prior-exec 56% each [M · T0-66 L139].

**Work footprint.** Application/deployment and orchestration paired with governance and product planes, concentrated in verticalised mid-market niches (n = 13: agency/martech 3, PE/financial 2, tech consulting 2, legal 1, general 5) [M · T0-66 L173].

**Decision rule.** Treat fractional AI leadership as a **senior, network- and bench-sourced career**, never a job-board search [B · T0-66 L70–72]. Sell concrete 90-day outcomes (a scoped pilot with a measured KPI plus a 12-month roadmap) rather than the "Fractional CAIO" title alone [D · T0-18 L1300]. Full launch gates, retainer math, templates and contract kits live in [Freelance and fractional practice](../../11-tracks/freelance-fractional.md).

## Employer landscape and title confusion

**Nearly every public "posting" is a bench, advisor network or search-firm template—not an open vacancy.** Across ~100 search results reviewed in T0-66, only **13 qualifying postings** emerged (US 7, UK 5, Canada 1, India 0), and **only 1 of 13 (7.7%)** sat on an employer's own careers page (*OneSeven Tech*, posted 2026-03-10 and still open on 2026-11-13 as a bench-building role) [A/M · T0-66 L52, L107–112, L528, L700–701]. The other 12 sat on advisor networks or staffing firms (5), executive-search firm Axial Search (4 templated pages explicitly stating they are *"for future opportunities"*, not a live role), and managed marketplaces (3 across Fractionus and Go Fractional) [B/M · T0-66 L52, L119, L529–540]. Arootah similarly warns that joining its advisor network carries no live engagement or placement guarantee [B · T0-66 L52, L160, L529]. Moreover, **3 of 7 distinct fractional AI postings** examined across Go Fractional, Built In and Fractionus were already closed when opened, and Go Fractional's home page displayed `"0 open roles"` (likely a client-side counter), making live posting volume unverifiable [B/E · T0-66 L52, L113–114, L341, L702].

**Boundary with full-time [VP / Head of AI](vp-head-of-ai.md) and [Chief AI Officer](chief-ai-officer.md) (reconciling T0-65 and T0-66):**
- Full-time corporate CAIO seats add formal board attendance and sign-off authority (and are largely filled internally or via retained search) [C, COI · T0-65 L67; T0-66 L172].
- Fractional seats operate with *limited formal authority* (Axial explicitly lists comfort operating fractionally with limited authority) and often convert to full-time or transition out once an organisation moves from strategy and pilot design into sustained execution [B/C, COI · T0-66 L172, L537].
- **Watch for title inflation and vertical gates.** Practitioners report title inflation on both buyer and seller sides [C, COI · T0-66 L176], and vertical mandates can be strict (Estrin VIP makes a law-firm background mandatory; UK agency roles require HubSpot CRM or marketing-ops attribution depth [B · T0-66 L75, L531–532, L535]).
- **Aliases observed across T0-65 and T0-66:** *Fractional Chief AI Officer (Fractional CAIO)*, *Fractional Head of AI*, *AI Strategy Leader*, *AI Value Creation Leader*, *AI Delivery Engine Leader*, and *Fractional CTO* with an AI-strategy remit in closed listings (note: *Fractional AI Architect* from T0-18 did not surface in T0-66's posting sample) [B · T0-65 L570; T0-66 L340, L528–540].

## Career path, India demand and market signals

**Preparation notes (curated evidence, no model answers per D-018):**
- **Proof of work that converts:** Prepare **2+ shipped production AI projects**, each tied to a single verifiable business metric, formatted as client-ready case studies (pattern: *led [initiative] from discovery to deployment, delivering [metric] in [period]*) [A/B/P · T0-66 L74, L169, L175, L724].
