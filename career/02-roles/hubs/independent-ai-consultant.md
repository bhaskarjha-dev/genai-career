## What the role actually looks like

An Independent AI Consultant or Contractor sells AI engineering builds or advisory engagements directly to clients—or through staffing intermediaries—on hourly, daily or fixed-fee project terms [B · T0-66 L240]. Across the November 2026 dossier's 17 qualifying contract postings (US 8, UK 8, India 1) and the UK IT Jobs Watch benchmark of 1,466 GenAI contract adverts (six months to 2026-11-12), the bulk of advertised independent work is **hands-on GenAI delivery** rather than slide-deck strategy [B/M · T0-66 L55–56, L185, L245]:

| Work type in sample (n = 17) | Count | Typical deliverables & stack | Engagement structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hands-on GenAI / LLM build contracts** | 10 | Production Python LLM applications, RAG pipelines, LangGraph / MCP agentic workflows, evaluation/guardrails, AWS Bedrock / Azure AI Foundry deployments [B/M · T0-66 L245, L550–557] | 6–12 month contracts (US 1099/C2C/W2; UK inside- or outside-IR35 day-rate contracts) [B · T0-66 L240] |
| **Advisory and integration analyst roles** | 3 | Cross-functional AI implementation, hyperscaler/CX vendor selection, business-minded AI integration [B/M · T0-66 L245, L541–542, L544] | 6-month 1099 or negotiable project terms [B · T0-66 L541, L544] |
| **Domain, security and defence SMEs** | 3 | Nonprofit AI strategy, MOD/defence SC-cleared or air-gapped LLM builds, AI security/governance (NIST AI RMF, ISO 27001, OWASP LLM) [B/M · T0-66 L245, L548, L553–555] | Specialist 1099 or UK day-rate contracts; clearance/residence gates apply [B · T0-66 L246, L248] |
| **Adjacent contract data science** | 1 | 6-month extendable ML/NLP and API orchestration (India) [B/M · T0-66 L245, L549] | Fixed-term agency contract [B · T0-66 L549] |

**What skills the market actually buys.** Because the 17 sampled postings were snippet-heavy, UK IT Jobs Watch co-occurrence across **1,466 GenAI contract ads** (six months to 2026-11-12) provides the stronger cross-check [B · T0-66 L195–215, L618–629]: **Python (34.6%)**, **LLM (34.1%)**, **Azure (29.7%)**, **Machine Learning (28.5%)**, **stakeholder management (27.4%)**, **CI/CD (25.6%)**, **RAG (23.7%)**, **AWS (23.2%)**, **Agile (20.9%)**, **SQL (17.7%)**, **DevOps (15.5%)**, **MLOps / roadmaps / use-case work (13.5% each)** and **prompt engineering (12.5%)** [B · T0-66 L199–215]. In the live posting sample (n = 17), LLM apps appeared in 59% (10/17), Python, agents (LangGraph, MCP) and cloud in 35% each (6/17), RAG and ML fundamentals in 24% each (4/17), and evals in 18% (3/17, UK postings) [M · T0-66 L199–206, L601–607].

**Work footprint.** Application and deployment on Azure and AWS with RAG, agent orchestration and evaluation; hands-on engineering dominates over pure advisory [M · T0-66 L245].

**Decision rule.** Treat agency contract job boards as a *floor check* rather than your primary channel: agency postings compress rates dramatically compared with direct client engagements [B/C · T0-66 L77; T0-18 L400–404]. Full launch gates, pricing cards, client-acquisition funnels and contract clauses are owned by [Freelance and fractional practice](../../11-tracks/freelance-fractional.md).

## Employer landscape and title confusion

**Job boards are agency-mediated, not direct client pipelines.** Twelve of the 17 qualifying contract postings came from staffing agencies or recruiters, 3 from IT-services firms and 2 from other intermediaries [M · T0-66 L193]. In the UK, live LLM contract demand is heavily concentrated in London (31 of 49 live contract LLM ads on 2026-11-14; 21 hybrid or remote), and postings routinely carry **inside-IR35** tax terms, active **SC security clearance** or **UK-residence** requirements [B · T0-66 L186, L246, L248, L697]. (Inside- vs outside-IR35 classification is a statutory UK tax matter—check official HMRC guidance and take professional advice rather than relying on recruiter summaries [B · T0-66 L248].)

**Title confusion and traps to avoid:**
- **"AI Consultant" in India almost always means a salaried employee.** Across 17 India "AI consultant" search results reviewed in T0-66, **15 of 17 (88%)** were permanent employee roles at consultancies and IT-services firms (Capgemini, Genpact, Deloitte India, NTT DATA, Merkle, Affine, Thoughtworks; downgraded from T0-66's A-on-Extract ledger row `S65` to Grade B); only **2 of 17** were fixed-term contracts [B/M · T0-66 L57, L190, L500, L705].
- **W2 vs 1099 ambiguity and useless rate bands.** US staffing listings frequently mix W2 staffing-agency employment with 1099/C2C independent contracting, and occasionally post uninformative ranges spanning 10× (e.g., AJAIA `$30–$300/hr`)—ignore any band wider than 5× [B/M · T0-66 L56, L248, L542, L728].
- **Boundary with [Fractional Head of AI / Fractional CAIO](fractional-ai-leader.md).** Independent consultants deliver scoped builds, integrations or technical advisory; fractional leaders hold a recurring part-time executive seat owning roadmap, governance and team coaching across 1–3 clients [B · T0-66 L168, L240].
- **Aliases observed on contract postings:** *AI Integration Consultant*, *Senior AI Consultant*, *Generative AI Consultant*, *AI Engineer (Contract)*, *Senior Applied AI Engineer*, *Principal Forward Deployed Engineer*, *AI and Data SME*, *Snr Full Stack Python Engineer – Agentic Systems*, *1099 AI Industry Specialist* [B · T0-66 L345, L541–557].

## Career path, India demand and market signals

**Demand growth vs rate compression.** Two independent signals show surging volume without rising headline rates:
1. **UK contract volume tripled while the median stayed flat:** IT Jobs Watch recorded **1,466 UK contract ads citing Generative AI** in the six months to 2026-11-12 (2.62% of UK contract ads), up from 472 (1.34%) in 2025 and 176 (0.49%) in 2024—yet the median day rate held flat at **£550/day** across both 2025 and 2026, below 2024's £625 [B · T0-66 L55, L185, L224, L668–669, L695–696].
2. **US LinkedIn growth rank:** LinkedIn's *Jobs on the Rise 2026* (US, released 2026-01-07) ranked **AI consultants and strategists #2** (measuring three-year growth in roles started from 2023-01-01 to 2025-07-31, not total volume, and excluding interim roles) [B · T0-66 L63, L187, L698].

**Prerequisites and credentials.** Experience was stated in 3 of 17 contract postings (5 preferred; 6+; 6–10 years), and in 4–8+ years across excluded undated UK postings; 0 of 17 named a degree, and only **1 of 17** named a certification (*CAIC*, preferred only) [M · T0-66 L242–243, L541]. Direct independent practice still requires passing the network, pipeline and runway gate in [Freelance and fractional practice](../../11-tracks/freelance-fractional.md). Note that T0-66 could not trace the widely cited "92% find clients through networks" figure outside Go Fractional's sponsor survey—other freelancer surveys put network/referral sourcing at **53–75%**, and a 2026 Ruul report puts platforms first at 33% [C · T0-66 L46, L346, L521].

**Rates, marketplaces and contracts (pointers to owner).** All independent, contractor and marketplace rate tables—plus contract clauses and marketplace fee comparisons—are owned by [Freelance and fractional practice](../../11-tracks/freelance-fractional.md) (decision D-019). As T0-66 decision anchors (as of November 2026):
- **Agency postings vs direct asking rates:** US agency-posted contracts in T0-66 clustered at **$50–$80/hr** (four postings, including Voyage Advisory at $70–$80/hr—downgraded from T0-66's A-on-Extract `L672` to Grade B), far below T0-18's $130–$235/hr senior rate cards, which T0-66 could not test on public boards [B · T0-66 L56, L226, L671–674]. Live UK contract ads quoted **£500–£1,000/day** (senior-leaning picks) around the **£550/day** UK median (London £575, ex-London £530; P75 £675, P90 £788–£800) [B · T0-66 L221–225, L662–670].
- **Marketplaces:** Toptal (0% talent fee, $79/mo client subscription, estimated 30–60% markup, 24-month non-circumvention per terms analysis, live-verified 2026-11-19 [C]), Upwork (0–15% variable freelancer fee since 2025-05-01, live-verified 2026-11-19 [B/C]), Braintrust (0% talent, 10–15% client [C]), Contra (0% commission, payment fees on free tier [C]) and Fiverr (20% seller fee, live-verified 2026-11-19 [B], avoid for senior AI work) [B/C · T0-66 L64, L365–370, L734–738].
- **India independent reality & IP law pointer:** Domestic India independent AI consulting rates were not found (`E`), and domestic "AI consultant" listings are employee roles (`15/17`) [M/E · T0-66 L57, L227, L249]. Plan around export clients, read [India remote tax](../../09-compensation/india-remote-tax.md), and note **India's Copyright Act 1957 s.17 and s.19(2)/(5)–(6)** (downgraded from T0-66's Extract-only Grade A `S78` to Grade B and live-verified 2026-11-19): an independent contractor is the first owner of commissioned software, and a written IP assignment that omits duration or territory defaults to **five years** and **India only** (applied by the Delhi High Court in *Pine Labs v. Gemalto*) [B · T0-66 L65, L374, L741–742]. *This is a statutory pointer, not legal advice; engage counsel.*

## Interview themes and preparation notes

**Direct client-side selection is undocumented (`E`), while managed marketplaces screen rigorously.** T0-66 found no systematic documentation of direct client-side hiring interviews for independent AI consultants [E · T0-66 L234]. For managed marketplaces, Toptal's documented screen (competitor-sourced, Grade C) runs **four stages over 2–4 weeks** (<3% stated acceptance): (1) online application, (2) live English and communication screen, (3) technical interview or portfolio review, and (4) a **two-week paid test project** with a real client [C · T0-66 L66, L233, L722].

**Preparation notes (curated evidence, no model answers per D-018):**
- **Proof over credentials:** No sampled contract posting required a GitHub portfolio link, while managed networks (Toptal) screen via portfolio review and live client work; lead client pitches with concise case studies following the pattern *built [LLM system] on [cloud] for [client], measured by [metric]* [C/P · T0-66 L241, L247].
- **Agency contract screening:** Expect rapid technical vetting focused on the exact cloud and orchestration stack in the requisition (Azure AI Foundry or AWS Bedrock, Python, LangGraph/MCP, RAG evals) plus immediate start availability and right-to-work/clearance checks [B · T0-66 L246, L550–557].
- **Contract due diligence before starting:** Check IR35 status (UK), W2 vs 1099 classification (US), explicit duration and worldwide territory in Indian IP assignments, and marketplace non-circumvention windows (up to 24 months on Toptal) before signing [B/C · T0-66 L248–249, L727, L730].
