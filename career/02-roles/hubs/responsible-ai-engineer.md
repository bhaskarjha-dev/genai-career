## What the role actually looks like

This is a **thin, hands-on technical compliance engineering niche** — building runtime guardrails, LLM and agent evaluation pipelines, red-team harnesses, observability and audit-ready documentation mapped to frameworks such as the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 [A/C · T0-61 L92, L354, L414].
