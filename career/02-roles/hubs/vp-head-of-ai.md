## What the role actually looks like

Across the 11 text-visible postings in the November 2026 dossier (out of 15 sampled), nearly half are hands-on builders leading a first AI team, while the rest are cross-functional strategy, governance and operations leads [A/B/M · T0-65 L72, L212–214]:

## Employer landscape and title confusion

**Title inflation is the dominant risk.** "Head of AI" at an early-stage startup or small consultancy frequently denotes the *first AI hire* with zero team and no dedicated budget, rather than a VP-level executive seat [B · T0-65 L87, L278, L553]. Conversely, large enterprises split the remit across multiple titles: Accela's Head of AI Strategy & Operations works alongside a separate Senior Director for AI transformation and operations [A · T0-65 L214, L545], and Synchrony's VP seat is explicitly a programme-management operating-engine role [B · T0-65 L214, L547].

**Boundaries with adjacent leadership roles:**
- **Vs [Chief AI Officer (CAIO)](chief-ai-officer.md).** VP / Head of AI and CAIO are distinct seats [B · T0-65 L278]. Critically, US federal **OMB Memorandum M-25-21** (issued 2025-04-03) requires federal agencies to designate a *CAIO*, **not** a VP or Head of AI—linking OMB M-25-21 as a Head of AI prerequisite in the older ontology card was a mis-mapping that T0-65 corrects [A · T0-65 L292, L383, L447].
- **Vs [AI Engineering Manager](ai-engineering-manager.md).** An AI EM leads an engineering team delivering a product or platform component; a Head of AI owns the company-wide AI function or strategy, though small-company player-coach Heads of AI overlap heavily with first-line EMs or founding staff engineers [B · T0-65 L132, L213].
- **Vs [Fractional Head of AI](fractional-ai-leader.md).** Mid-market firms and PE-backed portfolios often hire a fractional Head of AI for 90-day strategy/pilot sprints before converting the seat to a full-time VP/Head of AI once work moves to ongoing execution [C, COI · T0-66 L172].

**Employer mix and noisy demand indicators.** The 15 sampled postings covered 5 enterprise, 2 fintech, 2 AI-native, 1 scale-up, 2 consultancy/IT-services, 2 GCC-style and 1 undisclosed employer (seniority: 9 Head, 3 Director, 2 VP, 1 Senior Director; only 1 of 15 stated as remote) [M · T0-65 L119, L228–229]. Job-board counts are noisy upper bounds: Indeed US (~2,000 "VP of AI", undated, inflated by loose matches), Glassdoor US (505 "VP artificial intelligence", undated), Indeed India (300, noisy), Glassdoor India (296 "generative AI director", October 2026), and Fast AI Jobs (251 executive roles across 182 AI startups, mixing sales, ops and communications heads) [B · T0-65 L222–226, L676–680]. Executive search firm Axial Search's own tracker reported **65 US executive-level AI engineering postings over six months** to 2026-08-18 across Head of AI, VP and CAIO territory combined, concentrated in California, New York and Texas [C, COI · T0-65 L227, L276, L681].

**Aliases observed in postings:** *Head of AI Strategy & Operations*, *Head of AI Transformation*, *Senior Director – Head of AI*, *VP & GM Technology (Gen AI, Agentic AI)*, *Director of AI*, *Head of Applied AI*, *Director ML & AI Engineering*, *VP, Head of Program Management – AI*, *Head AI (India)*; Axial's talent network adds *VP, AI & Data*, *VP, AI Strategy* and *Director, AI Strategy* [B/C · T0-65 L216, L545–559].

## Interview themes and preparation notes

**Explicit evidence gap:** T0-65 found **no role-specific Head of AI interview-loop documentation** on employer boards or candidate-guide sites beyond generic recruiter notes [E · T0-65 L265, L399, L723]. Prepare by classifying which of the two variants you are interviewing for and combining the closest verified loop tracks from [interview loops](../../08-interviewing/interview-loops.md):

- **If the seat is a player-coach Head of AI:** Expect architecture and evaluation depth akin to a senior/staff AI Engineer plus AI EM leadership rounds—concrete walkthroughs of production LLM systems you shipped, how you wired evaluation into release cycles, and your first-hire plan [B/P · T0-65 L85, L271, L714].
- **If the seat is an enterprise strategy/enablement lead:** Prepare a structured portfolio and governance walkthrough—risk tiering, build-vs-buy trade-offs, adoption/ROI scorecards, regulatory translation (without claiming a framework where postings rarely name one), and concrete examples of driving delivery *without direct authority* [A/P · T0-65 L86, L214, L545, L715].
- **Due-diligence questions to ask the panel:** Confirm reporting line, engineering headcount vs matrixed influence, compute/vendor budget sign-off, and what 90-day success metrics distinguish a real function owner from title-inflated AI theatre [P · T0-65 L87, L278].
- **Proof and résumé framing:** State reporting line, team size, production systems shipped and the business metric moved; avoid bare technology lists detached from outcomes [P · T0-65 L277, L714–715]. (No model answers are given per D-018.)
