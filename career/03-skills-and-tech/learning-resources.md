Sequence your study using [learning roadmaps](learning-roadmaps.md) and target skills from [role skill inventories](role-skill-inventories.md).

Treat unverified placement percentages as unverified claims.
- **Rubric formula (Grade M):** `T = 4A + 4C + 4H + 5R + 3$ (0-5 per criterion, total out of 100; outcome adjustment and gates G1-G5 applied afterwards)` — Authority (20%), Currency (20%), Hands-on depth (20%), Role fit (25%), Cost (15%). Scores are tie-breakers, not job-outcome predictors [M · T0-68 L91, L121].
- **Hard gates override raw scores:**
  - **G1:** Currency: a fast-topic resource >18 months old is capped at use-with-care; material built on a retired API is avoid.
  - **G2:** Paid gate: above US$100 / INR 8,000, use requires a confirmed full-refund window >=14 days from the first session and traceable (or no) outcome claims.
  - **G3:** Conflict of interest (COI): vendor-authored, lab-authored or monetised material is explicitly flagged without score penalty.
  - **G4:** Standing and regulatory gate: seller-awarded titles without independent assessment are capped at watch; regulator/court adverse findings or closure mean avoid.
  - **G5:** Completeness gate: partial or unfinished courses with unpublished core units are capped at use-with-care.
- **Read-depth and COI disclosures:** Most T0-68 rows were rated from search extracts (`extract-only`, capped at Grade B under EGP-1.0); primary specifications added from T0-13, T0-16 and T0-69 are marked `Primary ref` rather than assigned a T0-68 rubric score. Vendor- or lab-authored resources are flagged `COI` — cross-check them with one independent source [B/C · T0-68 L22, L98].
- **Consumer-law and refund pointers** summarise documented terms and public commission/regulator records; they are pointers, not legal advice.
