# Stage 04: Certifications

> **The question this stage answers:** Is a certificate worth it for my target role and hiring channel, and which credential (if any) clears the bar?
> **Status:** Covered (partial Reduce Step R1 as of 2026-11-19): the decision guide, the 150-credential ranked catalog and the role × credential view are live. Hyperscaler (**T0-28**) and developer-tool (**T0-33**) RP2 sessions are deferred past 2026-12-05 and carried provisionally from T0-11, T3-01 and T3-02. Overall progress is tracked in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).
