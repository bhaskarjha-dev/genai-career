## How the value order works

Every credential in `data/credentials.json` is scored on six criteria as `[lo, mid, hi]` intervals (0–10) and ranked via deterministic Monte Carlo simulation (4,000 triangular draws, seed `20261119`) [M · decision D-026]. Read rank intervals (`P10–P90`) rather than point ranks: overlapping intervals mean two credentials are indistinguishable given current evidence.

| Criterion | Base weight | Meaning |
|---|:---:|---|
| `hiring_signal` | 30% | Measured share in large systematic job-posting audits (0–10 calibrated to the observed market ceiling where ~10% of target-role postings = 8.5–9.5). Left null in data/credentials.json whenever only small convenience samples or vendor claims exist; scripts/rank_credentials.py applies the conservative triangular prior [2.5, 4.0, 5.5] on the full weight vector so empirically measured credentials are never penalised relative to unmeasured peers (D-026 calibration, 2026-11-19). |
| `channel_value` | 20% | Partner-programme tier counts, FDE gates, SI/client staffing clauses and employer capability mandates. |
| `rigour` | 15% | Proctoring, live hands-on lab/practical assessment, public verification, renewal rules and resistance to brain-dumps or LLM assistance. |
| `skill_alignment` | 15% | Coverage of Must and Important skills for the target role families in data/skills_ontology.json. |
| `currency` | 10% | Blueprint recency (2025–2026 GenAI/agentic/governance updates) net of retirement or short-validity churn risk. |
| `cost_efficiency` | 10% | Inverse of exam/bundle fee and preparation-hour burden (lower cash and time cost scores higher). |
