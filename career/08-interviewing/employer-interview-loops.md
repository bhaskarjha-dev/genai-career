# Employer Interview Loops: 27 Employers, Policies and Timelines

> Per-employer interview loop structures across 27 frontier labs, Big Tech, AI-native startups, banks, consultancies and India or IT-services firms, with per-cell sources, AI-assistant policies, take-homes, in-person rules, research loops and timelines.

**Dates and grades.** Loop tables combine [T0-46](../../research/sessions/T0-46-interview-loops-genai-employers-2026.md) (retrieved 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-14, 25 employers across 31 loops), [T0-47](../../research/sessions/T0-47-ai-mediated-hiring-screening-interviews-fraud.md) (retrieved 2026-11-13, employer AI-policy enforcement) and two carry-forward Big Tech rows (Stripe and Uber) from [T0-14](../../research/sessions/T0-14-ai-system-design-interview-patterns.md) (dated 2026-10-21). Re-verify by 2027-02-11 (90 days from T0-46's retrieval; re-check Stripe and Uber rows alongside [interview loops](interview-loops.md) by 2027-01-19). **Every row names its own source and grade** rather than relying on an umbrella attribution. Rows for Anthropic that rest on Anthropic's own pages carry a **COI** tag because the T0-46 and T0-47 author model is Anthropic's; key Anthropic primary pages were re-verified independently on 2026-11-19. **Grades** (EGP-1.0, defined in [CONTRIBUTING §3](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#3-writing-standard-egp-10-in-brief)): A primary or employer-published; B transparent secondary or large platform dataset; C prep-vendor guide, press summary or single candidate report; D forum report or judgement; E searched, nothing found.

> **How to use this page.** Use this reference alongside [interview loops](interview-loops.md) (the guide that owns loop classification, pass-rate bounds, debriefs and recruiter templates). Loops differ by level, team and track inside one employer [C · T0-46 L67, L448], so treat every Grade C row as a reported starting prior and confirm every round in writing with the recruiter.

## Take-home assignments

Take-homes appeared in **about 13% of hires across all roles** in Ashby's platform dataset (over 54 million applications, through 2026-03-31) [B · T0-46 L83, L235, L389], with none reported in the Big Tech or Indian IT-services loops read [C · T0-46 L235]. They concentrate in senior, staff, research and AI-native roles at 4–8 hours; only Cursor's Project Day is described as paid, and no employer-published scoring rubric was found [C/E · T0-46 L83, L235–245].

> **Security check before running any take-home repo.** The "Contagious Interview" malware campaign (active since at least December 2022; Microsoft March 2026 advisory) uses fake AI-sector coding tests and weaponised project files [A/B · T0-47 L62, L186]. Verify the recruiter's company domain and ATS link, and execute unknown repositories only inside a disposable VM or container [B · T0-47 L79].

## Evidence and limits

- **Primary vs third-party mix.** Only three employers in T0-46 published pages read directly: Anthropic (COI), OpenAI (silent on AI rules) and Canva (stale date 2025-06-11) [A · T0-46 L121, L484]. Meta's AI-assisted coding round is corroborated across CoderPad's own blog, interviewing.io and PracHub [A/C · T0-46 L66]. Most remaining employer rows rest on single prep-vendor guides or candidate reports (Grade C/D), whose underlying samples cannot be audited [C · T0-46 L503].
- **Anthropic COI disclosure.** Anthropic's policy, take-home and pricing pages are primary (Grade A) but tagged COI because the session author model is Anthropic's; Anthropic's outcome claims about its take-home are not relied on, and Anthropic's primary pages were independently re-verified on 2026-11-19 [A, COI · T0-46 L508].
- **Known absences (Grade E).** No loop sources found for: the governance interview track (only fragments at Goldman Sachs and Cognizant); Anthropic p95 latency debugging in T0-46 (carries 1 report in T0-14); seven frontier labs (Mistral, Cohere, Microsoft AI, Thinking Machines, SSI, Meta Superintelligence Labs, Hugging Face); Indian AI labs, Indian product firms, named GCCs and Indian aptitude-test vendors; employer-published pass rates; and employer-published proctoring rules [E · T0-46 L479–495].
- **What would change this table.** Additional employers publishing official AI-use policies or loop guides; verified Indian AI-lab or GCC loop reports; governance-track interview evidence; resolution of Cursor's work stage and Databricks's AI-FDE customer round.
