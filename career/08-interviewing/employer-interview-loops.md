# Employer Interview Loops: 61 Employers, Policies and Timelines

> Per-employer interview loop structures across 61 frontier labs, Big Tech, AI-native startups, inference clouds, safety organisations, robotics labs, banks, consultancies, India and IT-services firms, and expert platforms, with per-cell sources, AI-assistant policies, take-homes, in-person rules, research loops and timelines.

**Dates and grades.** Loop tables combine [T0-46](../../research/sessions/T0-46-interview-loops-genai-employers-2026.md) (retrieved 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-14, 25 employers across 31 loops), [T0-47](../../research/sessions/T0-47-ai-mediated-hiring-screening-interviews-fraud.md) (retrieved 2026-11-13, employer AI-policy enforcement), two carry-forward Big Tech rows (Stripe and Uber) from [T0-14](../../research/sessions/T0-14-ai-system-design-interview-patterns.md) (2026-10-21), and 115 employer loop records harvested from RP2 role dossiers [T0-48](../../research/sessions/T0-48-role-dossier-ai-engineer.md) through [T0-66](../../research/sessions/T0-66-role-group-independent-fractional-contract.md) (retrieved 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-14). Re-verify by 2027-02-11 (90 days from T0-46's retrieval; re-check Stripe and Uber rows alongside [interview loops](interview-loops.md) by 2027-01-19). **Every row names its own source and grade** rather than relying on an umbrella attribution. Rows for Anthropic that rest on Anthropic's own pages carry a **COI** tag because the RP2 author model is Anthropic's; key Anthropic primary pages were re-verified independently on 2026-11-19. **Grades** (EGP-1.0, defined in [CONTRIBUTING §3](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#3-writing-standard-egp-10-in-brief)): A primary or employer-published; B transparent secondary or large platform dataset; C prep-vendor guide, press summary or single candidate report; D forum report or judgement; E searched, nothing found.

> **How to use this page.** Use this reference alongside [interview loops](interview-loops.md) (the guide that owns loop classification, pass-rate bounds, debriefs and recruiter templates). Loops differ by level, team and track inside one employer [C · T0-46 L67, L448], so treat every Grade C or D row as a reported starting prior and confirm every round in writing with the recruiter.
