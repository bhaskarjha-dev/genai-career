# Visa Dossier: Asia-Pacific

> [!CAUTION]
> **Pointers to official sources, not legal or immigration advice.** Several Asia-Pacific jurisdictions have major thresholds taking effect between `2026-12-01` and `2027-04-01` (Malaysia DE Rantau, Singapore EP and ONE Pass AI & Tech, Japan PR guidelines). Confirm every requirement on the national portal (`mom.gov.sg`, `immi.homeaffairs.gov.au`, `immigration.govt.nz`, `goldcard.nat.gov.tw`, `immd.gov.hk`, `mdec.my`) and with licensed local counsel before acting.

For upcoming cut-overs and pending rules in this region (Singapore `2027-01-01` EP floor, `2027-01-27` Tech.Pass closure, `2027-01-28` ONE Pass AI & Tech launch, Japan HSP income ordinance & `2027-04-01` PR guidelines, Taiwan 2025 Act implementing rules, Australia MATES 2026–27 ballot, NZ–India FTA TEE launch, and Malaysia DE Rantau `2026-12-01` reopening), check the [central V0 status table](../visa-and-relocation.md#status-table).

## Singapore: explicit AI shortage list and January 2027 rule shifts

- **Employment Pass (EP) & COMPASS:** Minimum fixed monthly salary rises from **S$5,600** (S$6,200 finance) to **S$6,000** (S$6,600 finance) — scaling with age to S$11,500/S$12,700 — for new applications from **`2027-01-01`** and renewals expiring from `2028-01-01` (`T0-42 L63, L153, L161, L615`; confirmed live `2026-11-19` on `mom.gov.sg`, `[A]`). Applicants must score **≥40 points on COMPASS** (exempt at ≥S$22,500 fixed/month); note that **C3 nationality diversity scores 0 points** if an applicant's nationality already makes up ≥25% of the employer's PMET workforce (`T0-42 L63, L74, L87`, `[A]`).
- **AI & ML on the Shortage Occupation List (SOL):** Effective `2026-01-01`, MOM's SOL explicitly names **AI engineer, AI researcher/scientist, data scientist, and software developer** — awarding bonus COMPASS points and unlocking a **5-year EP** (at S$10,700–14,270 in 2026; S$11,500–15,499 from `2027-01-01`) provided the employer scores ≥10 on C3 diversity (`T0-42 L63, L154`, `[A]`). Online EP decisions target **10 business days** (`T0-42 L63, L153`, `[A]`).
- **ONE Pass AI & Tech track & Tech.Pass sunset:** Standard **ONE Pass** requires S$30,000/month fixed salary over the past 12 months (or offered) at an established firm (≥US$500m market cap or ≥US$200m revenue); fees S$105 + S$225 (+S$30 MJV), MOM processing **"within 4 weeks for most cases"** (revising T0-08's 1–2 months) (`T0-42 L62, L141–L144, L155, L171, L395`, `[A]`). On **`2027-01-27` at 23:59**, EDB's **Tech.Pass closes permanently** (no automatic transfer); on **`2027-01-28`**, MOM opens the **ONE Pass AI & Tech track** requiring ≥S$30,000/month total (≥S$22,500 fixed + vested ESOP/ESOW) plus ≥5 years in 10 in founder/C-suite/tech roles at a qualifying tech company (≥US$500m valuation, US$200m revenue, US$500m AUM, or ≥US$30m startup raised) (`T0-42 L62, L64, L156–L157, L165–L166, L616–L617`, `[A/B]`).
- **PR reality:** EP and S Pass holders may apply directly to ICA (S$100, reviewed within 6 months, `2026-10-30`), but approval is **discretionary** with no published formula (`T0-42 L65, L619`, `[A]`).

## Australia and New Zealand: verified 2026–2027 thresholds

- **Australia Skills in Demand (SID 482) — conflict resolved live `2026-11-19`:** Although `T0-42` noted two vendor alerts citing AUD 146,717 / AUD 79,499, live verification against `immi.homeaffairs.gov.au` on `2026-11-19` confirms the binding **2026–2027** (1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027) Home Affairs floors are **AUD 146,576** for the **Specialist Skills Income Threshold (SSIT)** (no occupation list, ANZSCO 1/2/4/5/6, 50%/90% processing **37d / 78d** as of `2026-09-29`) and **AUD 79,423** for the **Core Skills Income Threshold (CSIT)** (Core Skills Occupation List, 4–9 months) (`T0-42 L70, L275–L276, L290–L291, L630`; verified `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Visa Application Charge rose on `2026-07-01` to **AUD 4,015** (main applicant) plus employer SAF levy (AUD 1,200 or 1,800/yr); **ENS 186 TRT** provides a 2-year PR pathway with a sponsor (`T0-42 L70, L278, L284, L631`, `[B]`).
- **Australia NIV (858) & MATES (403):** The invitation-only **National Innovation Visa (subclass 858)** replaced the Global Talent Independent programme and sits under Ministerial Direction 120 (`2026-07-25`) (`T0-42 L70, L277, L632`, `[B]`). **MATES (subclass 403)** is an **India-exclusive** ballot (ages 18–30, 3,000 places/year, AUD 25 ballot fee; 2026–27 ballot dates still unannounced as of `2026-11-19`) (`T0-42 L70, L74, L279, L612, L633`, `[B/C]`).
- **New Zealand Green List & India FTA — conflict resolved live `2026-11-19`:** Live verification on `immigration.govt.nz` (`2026-11-19`, `[B]`) confirms **Software Engineer (ANZSCO 261313)** sits on **Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence)** at **NZ$72.80/hour** (~NZ$151,424/yr on a 40-hour week), resolving T0-42's NZ$72.80/h vs NZ$120k vendor conflict (`T0-42 L71, L305`). **AEWV** removed the median-wage floor and redesigned the job check (5.5–7 weeks, NZ$1,540 worker fee), and **Skilled Migrant Category** updates took effect `2026-08-24` (`T0-42 L71, L303, L634–L635`, `[B]`). The **India–NZ FTA** entered into force `2026-10-20`, creating the 5,000-holder-cap **Temporary Employment Entry (TEE / India Skilled Work)** pathway (up to 3 years, **no direct automatic residency path** — prefer Green List Tier 1 if targeting PR) (`T0-42 L71, L306, L614, L636`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[B]`).

## East Asia & Malaysia: fast merit tracks and remote bases

- **Japan:** **J-Skip** (Master's or 10y experience + **¥20 million/yr**, or 5y management + ¥40m) and **HSP (80+ points)** retain **1-year PR** (70+ points → 3-year PR), while **J-Find** offers top-100 university graduates (≤5 years) up to 2 years of job search (`T0-42 L66, L185–L186, L204`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Note two major 2026–2027 tightenings: Japan's PR approval fee jumped from ¥10,000 to **¥200,000** on `2026-10-01` (`[B]`), and revised PR guidelines (stricter pension/income checks and Japanese B1) take effect mostly `2027-04-01` (`[C]`), alongside a proposed ordinance raising HSP's ¥3m income floor by `2027-03-31` (`T0-42 L66, L191–L192, L620–L622`). Japan's Digital Nomad Visa **excludes Indian citizens** (`T0-42 L74, L187`, `[B]`).
- **Taiwan:** The unsponsored **Employment Gold Card** (open work permit, ~7–14 weeks `[M]`) qualifies high earners at **≥NT$160,000/month** average salary in any year within the past 3 years (documented as "salary" on official tax statements; **software and AI engineers are advised to apply under the Digital field**, not Science & Technology Reg. 1) (`T0-42 L68, L231, L242, L604`, `[A/M]`). 2025 Foreign Professionals Act amendments enable spouse work permits and 1-year PR at ≥NT$6m + master's, though implementing rules remain unverified (`T0-42 L68, L231, L626`, `[B]`).
- **Hong Kong:** **TTPS** Category A (≥HK$2.5m/yr, 36m) and Categories B/C (200-university list, 24m; ~110,000 approvals to July 2025) offer fast unsponsored entry, but **extension strictly requires an active Hong Kong job or operating business** (`T0-42 L69, L254, L356, L609`, `[B]`). September 2026 Policy Address proposals (easier founder extensions, AI on the Talent List) were not yet live on ImmD pages (`T0-42 L69, L629`, `[B]`).
- **South Korea & Malaysia:** Korea's **Top-Tier Visa (`F-2-T`)** (>3× GNI = ₩149.865m in 2025, 3y to F-5 PR) targets senior advanced-tech hires, while **F-1-D Workation** became permanent on `2026-06-30` and is open to Indians (`T0-42 L67, L213–L220, L624–L625`, `[B]`). Malaysia doubled **EP basic salary floors** on `2026-06-01` (Cat I **MYR 20,000+**, Cat II MYR 10k–19,999) and reopens its **DE Rantau Nomad Pass** (explicitly naming AI/ML/data as Tech Talent) on **`2026-12-01`** at **USD 36,000/yr** for up to **4 years** (no appeals since `2026-08-01`; no PR path; taxable from day 61) (`T0-42 L72, L328–L341, L637–L639`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A/B]`).

## Decision rules by profile

- **Mid-level engineer with an offer:** **Singapore EP** (explicit AI Shortage Occupation List, 10-business-day online processing — verify the employer's COMPASS C3 nationality diversity score first) and **Australia SID 482 Specialist Skills** (AUD 146,576+, 37–78d processing, 2-year ENS 186 PR path) are the top institutional hubs (`T0-42 L63, L70`). For immediate PR with an offer, **New Zealand Green List Tier 1** (Software Engineer at NZ$72.80/h) beats every other APAC timeline (`T0-42 L71`).
- **Senior / Staff engineer without an offer:** **Taiwan Gold Card** (≥NT$160k/mo prior salary, Digital field, open work permit) and **Hong Kong TTPS** (Cat A ≥HK$2.5m or top-200 university, mindful of the job-on-renewal rule) provide unsponsored entry; executives/founders clearing S$30k total with ≥S$22.5k fixed should prepare for **Singapore ONE Pass AI & Tech** opening `2027-01-28` (`T0-42 L62, L68–L69`).
- **Remote contractor from India:** **Malaysia DE Rantau** (reopening `2026-12-01` at USD 36,000/yr, up to 4 years) and **South Korea F-1-D Workation** accept Indian citizens — whereas **Japan's Digital Nomad Visa excludes India** (`T0-42 L67, L72, L74`).

## Pitfalls and vendor myths

- **Applying to Singapore Tech.Pass in late 2026/2027 without checking sunset:** Tech.Pass closes permanently `2027-01-27 23:59` with **no automatic conversion** to ONE Pass AI & Tech (`T0-42 L64, L617`, `[B]`).
- **Ignoring Singapore COMPASS C3 diversity for Indian hires:** Small/mid-sized tech offices in Singapore where ≥25% of PMETs share one nationality score 0 on C3, requiring either ≥S$22,500 fixed salary (COMPASS-exempt) or strong C1/C2/SOL bonus points (`T0-42 L63, L74, L87`, `[A]`).
- **Using Taiwan's online Travel Authorization Certificate to enter and switch to Gold Card:** Indian nationals entering on a TAC cannot file domestically; apply from abroad or enter on an eligible visa (`T0-42 L74, L374`, `[A]`).
- **Assuming NZ–India FTA TEE gives automatic PR:** The 5,000-holder TEE visa runs up to 3 years with **no direct PR path** (`T0-42 L71, L306`; verified `2026-11-19`, `[B]`).
