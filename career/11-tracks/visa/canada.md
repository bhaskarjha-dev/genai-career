## Express Entry: the November 2026 CRS overhaul

- **High-wage Labour Market Integration points (`2026-11-03`):** IRCC replaced the March 2025 abolition of LMIA job-offer points with a new **Labour Market Integration** factor rewarding high-wage qualifying job offers or Canadian experience **with no LMIA required** (`T0-38 L51`; confirmed live `2026-11-19` on `canada.ca`, `[A]`):
  - **NOC 20012** (Computer and information systems managers, ≥2× national median): **+100** qualifying offer / **+70** Canadian experience.
  - **NOC 21231** (Software engineers and designers) and **NOC 21211** (Data scientists, ≥1.5× median): **+70** qualifying offer / **+40** Canadian experience.
  - **NOC 21234** (Web developers, ≥1.3× median): **+50** qualifying offer / **+20** Canadian experience (`T0-38 L51, L70`, `[A]`).
- **Pause and pre-reset draw baselines:** IRCC paused invitation rounds from `2026-11-03` while recalculating all pool scores, and added researcher category refinements effective `2026-11-17`; as of `2026-11-19`, post-reset CEC cut-offs have not yet published (`T0-38 L52`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[A/B]`). Before the pause, October 2026 cut-offs stood at **519** for CEC (`2026-10-15`; range 516–523 Aug–Oct), **407** for French proficiency (`2026-10-27`, 5,000 ITAs), 476 for Trades, and 477 for Transport (`T0-38 L53, L170–L173`, `[A]`).
- **No STEM draw since 2024:** IRCC held **zero STEM category-based draws in 2025 or 2026 through `2026-11-19`**, and no general all-programme draw was found in 2026; STEM-occupation ITAs fell from 24,084 (2023) to 13,749 (2024) to 5,942 (2025) (`T0-38 L53`, `[A]`). Offshore GenAI applicants without Canadian experience, a qualifying Canadian job offer, or NCLC 7+ French cannot rely on a STEM draw.
- **2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan (`2026-11-05`):** Sets **380,000 PRs/year** (100,000 Federal High Skilled, 95,000 PNP), **220,000 temporary workers/year** (170,000 IMP, 50,000 TFWP), and **150,000 new international students/year** (down from 408,000 total 2026 study permits including extensions; Master's/PhD at public DLIs remain exempt from Provincial Attestation Letters) (`T0-38 L61, L236`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[A]`). A time-limited measure announced `2026-11-12` targets up to 100,000 in-Canada workers for PR via extra PNP allocations through `2027-03-31` (`T0-38 L61, L139`, `[B]`).

## Global Talent Stream, employer reality, and provincial changes

- **GTS timing and volume:** ESDC processed GTS LMIAs in an average of **9 business days** in September 2026 (versus 98 days for standard high-wage LMIAs, `[A · T0-38 L50]`). However, IRCC explicitly warns that its two-week Global Skills Strategy visa target will "probably take more than two weeks" (`T0-38 L50`, `[A]`), and work-permit processing from India stood at **10 weeks** on `2026-11-04` (`T0-38 L50`, `[B]`) — making **1–3 months** offer-to-start the realistic planning window (revising T0-08's 1–2 months).
- **Employer scrutiny and declining GTS volume:** GTS represented only **2,384 positions** (1.6% of 146,531 LMIAs) between April 2025 and March 2026 — roughly 30% of its 2022 pace (`T0-38 L58, L200`, `[C/M]`). An ESDC eligibility update on `2026-09-18` bars independent-contractor misclassification, tightening scrutiny on IT staffing and outsourcing firms (`T0-38 L135, L160, L253`, `[A]`). Prevailing wages refresh annually on Job Bank (scheduled `2026-11-18`; confirm exact NOC median before filing, `T0-38 L49, L160`).
- **Provincial retrenchment:**
  - **British Columbia:** Closed its Tech Pilot, graduate, and entry-level streams in April 2026; its `2026-10-29` Innovate draw required either a **C$49/hour and C$99,000/year** wage floor or 131 points (`T0-38 L56, L112, L182`, `[B]`).
  - **Ontario:** Closed eight OINP streams in June 2026 (including Human Capital Priorities Tech draws and Master's/PhD Graduate streams) and consolidated into **Ontario Workforce Priority**, which requires a job offer (EOI opened `2026-08-04`) (`T0-38 L56, L113, L183`, `[B]`).
  - **Quebec:** Ended the Programme de l'expérience québécoise (PEQ) on `2025-11-19` and its AI/IT/VFX permanent immigration pilot on `2026-01-01`, leaving PSTQ via Arrima plus a French-language requirement on TFW renewals after 3 years (`2025-12-17`) (`T0-38 L56, L114, L184`, `[B]`).

## Indian-national specifics

- **No nationality cap:** Indian citizens received **43.5%** of all Express Entry ITAs in 2024 (43,004 of 98,903) with no per-country backlog (`T0-38 L59`, `[A]`).
- **Processing & documents:** Work-permit applications from India averaged 10 weeks (`2026-11-04`, `[B]`); Express Entry requires an Educational Credential Assessment (e.g., WES, valid 5 years; none required for GTS) and a Police Clearance Certificate via Passport Seva (`T0-38 L213, L235`, `[A]`).
- **Trade talks:** India–Canada CEPA Round 5 took place in October 2026; no mobility provisions are in force as of `2026-11-19` (`T0-38 L59, L211`, `[B]`).
- **US-to-Canada H-1B hoppers:** The Budget 2025 H-1B accelerated pathway has **not launched** as of `2026-11-19` (consultations closed `2026-09-01`; live check `[B]`). H-1B holders must use GTS, ICT, Express Entry, or a job-offer PNP (`T0-38 L54, L118, L254`, `[A/B]`).

## Remote work and tax pointer

- **Digital-nomad visitor entry:** Foreign nationals may work remotely for a **non-Canadian** employer while visiting Canada for up to **6 months** on visitor status, subject to stricter May 2026 foreign-income proof checks; PGWP and open-permit rules do not automatically resolve foreign-payroll compliance (`T0-38 L221`, `[A/B]`). Vendor claims of a "1-year Canadian digital nomad visa" conflict with official visitor stay limits (`T0-38 L221`, `[C]`).
- **Tax residency:** Determined by CRA residential ties and days present (`canada.ca/taxes`; India–Canada tax treaty may apply; pointers, not tax advice, `T0-38 L225`).

## Decision rules by profile

- **Mid-level engineer with a Canadian offer:** Confirm whether the employer will file **GTS Category B** (NOC 21231 / 21211) or **ICT C12**, and budget **1–3 months** for visa-office issuance from India (`T0-38 L50, L105, L107`). Once in Canada (or even with a qualifying high-wage offer under NOC 21231/21211), the `2026-11-03` +70/+40 CRS points materially boost Express Entry eligibility — watch the first post-reset CEC cut-offs (`T0-38 L51–L52`).
- **Senior practitioner without an offer:** Do not expect a STEM Express Entry draw or a provincial Master's/HCP draw (`T0-38 L53, L56`). Your two realistic no-offer PR levers are **French at NCLC 7+** (October 2026 cut-off 407) or landing a high-wage Canadian job offer directly via GTS/ICT (`T0-38 L51, L53`).
- **Recent graduate:** Public-DLI Master's and PhD programmes remain exempt from Canada's PAL study-permit cap and qualify for PGWP (CLB 7), though Ontario and BC graduate PNP shortcuts closed in 2026 — plan on CEC + high-wage NOC points instead (`T0-38 L56, L62`).

## Pitfalls and vendor myths

- **Assuming GTS has "no merit test" across the board:** Prior Phase 0 guidance (`T0-08`) omitted the Category A distinction — Category B has no worker merit test, whereas Category A requires a designated-partner referral, a "unique and specialised" test, and a `C$80k`/`C$150k` salary floor (`T0-38 L104–L105, L253`, `[A]`).
- **Waiting for an H-1B visa or "20-day AI work permit":** Neither the Budget 2025 H-1B pathway nor the June 2026 National AI Strategy GTS expansion had published operational rules as of `2026-11-19` (`T0-38 L54–L55, L297`, `[A/B]`).
- **Relying on the Start-Up Visa:** Paused to new applicants since `2026-06-30`; treat any vendor still selling Start-Up Visa packages with extreme scepticism (`T0-38 L57, L141`, `[A]`).
