# Visa Dossier: Gulf States & Israel

> [!CAUTION]
> **Pointers to official sources, not legal or immigration advice.** Gulf residency rules frequently differ between federal portals (`icp.gov.ae`, `u.ae`) and emirate portals (`gdrfad.gov.ae`, `adro.gov.ae`), and several Saudi, Qatari, and Israeli implementing details remain unverified in official English text. Confirm directly with the sponsoring employer's PRO and licensed local counsel before relocating.

For contested and pending rules in this region (UAE Golden Visa basic-vs-gross salary enforcement, UAE ADRO Green Visa nomination pause, Saudi 2025 skill-classification thresholds, Qatar Labour Law No. 9/2026 freelance frameworks, and Israel B/1 Hi-Tech spouse work-permit renewal post-`2026-03-01`), check the [central V0 status table](../visa-and-relocation.md#status-table).

## United Arab Emirates

- **Standard employment & Emiratisation:** Most GenAI hires enter on a 2-year employer-sponsored MoHRE work permit (skilled tier ≥AED 4,000/mo + attested degree; employer fee AED 50 + AED 250/1,200/3,450 by firm category; 2 working days) (`T0-41 L56, L110, L141`, `[B]`). Emiratisation rules levy penalties on mainland firms of 50+ staff (AED 6,000/mo per missed target, rising AED 1,000/yr through 2026) and 20–49 staff (AED 108,000 in January 2026), pushing smaller firms to weigh free-zone entities (`T0-41 L60, L131, L143`, `[B]`).
- **Golden Visa (10-year skilled professional):** Requires a valid UAE employment contract, MoHRE occupational level 1 or 2, an attested Bachelor's degree (with Ministry of Education equivalency), and **≥AED 30,000/month** (`T0-41 L56, L111`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Holders sponsor spouses, children of any age, and parents (`T0-41 L56, L111`, `[B]`).
  - **Contested pay basis (V0):** Major corporate law firms (Fragomen `2024-06-13`; Vialto `2025-12-29`, `2026-06-12`; practitioner alerts `2026-11-19`) report strict enforcement of **AED 30,000 basic salary** (excluding housing/transport allowances) plus 6-month bank statements (and in Dubai, 2-year pay history / tenure checks), whereas *The National* (`2025-03-31`) reported gross salary and Abu Dhabi's official Arabic ADRO text states "monthly salary" without specifying basic vs gross (`T0-41 L57, L130`, `[B]`). Negotiate ≥AED 30,000 **basic** salary in the MOHRE contract if Golden Visa eligibility matters.
  - **Fee correction to `T0-08`:** Prior Phase 0 guidance (`T0-08`) quoted AED 3,800–4,800 (vendor estimate); official GDRFA Dubai line items sum to **AED 1,640** (`[M]`, plus AED 100/year beyond 2 years, medical test, and Emirates ID — still below vendor package quotes) (`T0-41 L57, L181, L594`, `[B/M]`).
- **Green Visa, Job Exploration, and Virtual Working:** The 5-year **Green Visa** requires a valid UAE contract, MoHRE levels 1–3, Bachelor's degree, and ≥AED 15,000/mo (or AED 360,000/yr freelancer income over 2 years), though Abu Dhabi's Arabic ADRO page marks nomination via ADRO unavailable (`T0-41 L56, L113–L114, L121`, `[B]`). The **Job-Exploration Visit Visa** is the only verified no-offer entry route (AED 1,000 refundable deposit + AED 200–400 fees, 48h; note ICP requires skilled level 1–3 *or* top-500 graduate for 60/90d, whereas GDRFA Dubai states *and* for 60/90/120d) (`T0-41 L58, L128`, `[B]`). The **Virtual Working Programme** requires **USD 3,500/month** from a non-UAE employer (1y renewable, 48h, ~AED 740 itemised) (`T0-41 L95, L117, L169`, `[B/M]`).
- **AI / Coders routes — what is verified vs marketing:** The National Program for Coders states a 100,000 Golden Visa goal, but neither `u.ae` (`2026-10-21`) nor ICP's guide (`2026-09-10`) publishes standalone coder criteria; Dubai's digital-tech talent permit requires Emirates Council for AI nomination (criteria unstated) (`T0-41 L59`, `[B]`). The `2025-09-29` AI-specialist announcement is a **visit visa**, not residence (`T0-41 L59, L116, L129`, `[B]`).

## Saudi Arabia

- **Standard employment & Nitaqat:** Managed via Qiwa under Labour Law Art. 40 (employer bears permit fees; monthly expatriate levy **SAR 700** where foreign headcount ≤ Saudi headcount or **SAR 800** where foreign > Saudi, plus SAR 100 licence — correcting the flat SAR 800 assumption) (`T0-41 L60, L220, L231`, `[B]`). Evolved Nitaqat (April 2026–December 2028) sets 30% Saudisation for engineering and 70% for project management (from `2027-02-14`), with no standalone IT quota stated in the legal summary (`T0-41 L60, L232–L233`, `[B]`).
- **2025 Skill Classification & Kafala:** Saudi Arabia introduced a 3-tier work-permit classification (High-Skilled / Skilled / Basic) in mid-2025 (effective-date reports conflict June/July vs July/August 2025), yet **no SAR salary thresholds, points tables, or differentiated entitlements** were published in verified official sources (`T0-41 L70, L236, L246`, `[B]`). Claims that *kafala* was fully abolished are **unconfirmed**: UN experts urged its abolition on `2026-04-29`, and the ILO Governing Body closed its Art. 26 complaint on `2026-11-16` on the basis of a 5-year workplan (measures due by end-2027; neither ILO text mentions *kafala*) (`T0-41 L61, L234–L235`, `[B]`).
- **Premium Residency:** Because `pr.gov.sa` failed to render Special Talent details, criteria rely on law-firm and MISA summaries (`[B]`): **Special Talent** (SAR 4,000 + USD 170, ~90 workdays, Nitaqat-exempt, employer mobility) targets researchers (≥SAR 14,000/mo), health/scientific/tech specialists (≥SAR 35,000/mo + Bachelor's + recommendation), and executives (≥SAR 80,000/mo), with no explicit "AI engineer" field named (`T0-41 L61, L221, L281`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Paid options (SAR 100,000/yr Limited; SAR 800,000 Unlimited) do not clearly state independent work rights on the portal (`T0-41 L61, L224`, `[B]`).

## Qatar and Bahrain

- **Qatar:** Entry remains strictly employer-sponsored under Law No. 21/2015 (1–5y validity) (`T0-41 L62, L296, L313`, `[B]`). Labour Law No. 9 of 2026 (`2026-06-25`) contemplates part-time and freelance work under forthcoming frameworks, and the March 2024 5-year talent/entrepreneur permit (QAR 4,000/5,000) remains unconfirmed in operational portal text (`T0-41 L62, L306, L316`, `[B]`). Property residency (QAR 730k tied / QAR 3.65M) states no independent work right (`T0-41 L62, L298`, `[B]`).
- **Bahrain:** Transparent LMRA overseas permits (BHD 195/390 + BHD 35, 21 working days; 2026–2029 fee schedule rises toward BHD 269/yr and monthly levy BHD 30) with strong job mobility (free transfer without employer consent after 12 months, 3 working days) (`T0-41 L63, L369–L373`, `[B]`). **Crucial caveat on Bahrain's Golden Residency:** The employee track (≥BHD 2,000/mo, BHD 305 every 10 years — refuting the "BHD 300/month levy" myth) requires **prior Bahrain residency** (not an entry visa) and **still requires an employer-applied LMRA work permit to work** (`T0-41 L63, L371, L380`, `[B]`).

## Israel

- **B/1 Foreign Expert & High-Tech Fast Track:** Under PIBA Procedure 5.3.0041 (10th ed., `2026-09-06`), **B/1 Expert** requires unique expertise and at least **2× the Israeli average wage** (**NIS 27,132/month** from `2026-01-01` per Meitar `2026-02-23`, statutory fees NIS 13,150/yr, 30 working days) (`T0-41 L64, L439, L450`, `[B]`). **Procedure 5.3.0043 (High-Tech Fast Track)** cuts processing to **6 working days** "where possible" for Innovation Authority-recognised firms; Israeli-university high-tech graduates qualify at 150% average wage from month 7 (500 annual cap) (`T0-41 L82, L439–L441`, `[B]`).
- **Hard limits & spousal V0 warning:** B/1 work visas carry a strict **63-month (5 years, 3 months) statutory cap** and **no permanent-residence path** (`T0-41 L64, L486`, `[B]`). Although Innovation Authority and law-firm guides still describe open spousal B/1 work authorisation (EAS, filed simultaneously), Procedure 5.3.0043's text stated the spousal arrangement ended `2026-03-01` and no post-`2026-03-01` statutory extension was verified on `gov.il` as of `2026-11-19` (only temporary 3-month emergency blanket visa extensions were confirmed) (`T0-41 L82, L440, L451`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Verify spousal work eligibility with Israeli counsel before accepting an offer.

## Indian-national specifics

- **No nationality quotas:** None of the five countries imposes Indian numerical caps; Indian degree holders are exempt from India's Emigration Check Required (ECR) gate (MEA 2016 rule; the Overseas Mobility Bill 2025 remains a draft) (`T0-41 L66, L83`, `[B]`).
- **Bilateral pacts do NOT cover GenAI:** Although India holds bilateral labour agreements with all five states, **none covers skilled IT or AI roles** (Israel's 2023/2026 protocols cover construction, caregiving, commerce, restaurants, and manufacturing) (`T0-41 L66`, `[B]`).
- **Attestation trap:** Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and Israel are Hague Apostille members (noting India's HCCH `A**` objection mark), whereas **the UAE and Qatar are NOT Hague Apostille members** — Indian degree and marriage certificates require MEA + Embassy + MOFA legalisation (budget 3–6 weeks before mobilisation) (`T0-41 L66, L158–L159, L259–L260`, `[A]`).

## Decision rules by profile

- **Mid-level or senior engineer optimising for 0% income tax and fast mobilisation:** **UAE** (employer MoHRE permit in 2 working days, upgrading to Golden Visa once basic pay clears AED 30,000/mo) is the lowest-friction Gulf base; **Saudi Arabia** offers strong packages on large AI/giga-project employers (verify Special Talent eligibility at ≥SAR 35k/mo if wanting mobility) (`T0-41 L56–L61`).
- **Remote engineer with USD/global income:** **UAE Virtual Working** (USD 3,500/mo from a non-UAE employer, 48h) provides a clean 1-year renewable base (`T0-41 L95, L117`).
- **Researcher or specialist considering Israel:** High-Tech Fast Track (6 working days) works well for 1–5 year stints at recognised R&D labs, but treat it as strictly temporary (63-month cap, no PR) and verify spousal EAS work-permit status in writing first (`T0-41 L64, L82`).

## Pitfalls and vendor myths

- **"AED 100,000 nomination-based UAE Golden Visa for Indians":** Publicly contradicted by ICP in July 2025 (`T0-41 L69, L133–L134`, `[B]`).
- **"UAE 3-year AI Specialist Residence Visa":** Unverified vendor invention (`[E]`); the `2025-09-29` announcement is a short-term **visit visa** only (`T0-41 L59, L69, L129`, `[B]`).
- **"Bahrain BHD 300/month Golden Visa levy":** False — BHD 300 is a **10-year renewal fee** (plus BHD 5 application), and the Golden Residency still requires an employer LMRA work permit to work (`T0-41 L63, L380`, `[B]`).
- **"Saudi Arabia abolished kafala":** Overstated; ILO's `2026-11-16` closure rests on a multi-year workplan extending to end-2027, and sponsorship mechanics remain live (`T0-41 L61, L234–L235`, `[B]`).

## Evidence and limits

- **Retrieval coverage (`T0-41`):** Every source in `T0-41` (87/87) was retrieved via fetch summaries rather than raw verbatim HTML/PDF inspection, and several portals (`pr.gov.sa` Special Talent, Qatar Hukoomi fee tables) did not render (`T0-41 L4`). Accordingly, all non-apostille statutory figures are capped at Grade `[B]`, cross-checked live on `2026-11-19`.
- **Absences:** No Gulf or Israeli ministry publishes GenAI-specific visa counts; Israel's only PIBA high-tech country breakdown surfaced is stale (2018–2019: 275 arrivals, 46% US, 23% India, `T0-41 L65, L463`).
