# Visa Dossier: Nordics, Southern & Central Europe

> [!CAUTION]
> **Pointers to official sources, not legal or immigration advice.** National salary thresholds, digital-nomad income multiples, and EU directive transpositions shift annually. Confirm every rule on the national immigration authority's portal and with local counsel before accepting an offer or relocating.

For pending changes across these ten jurisdictions (Sweden's `2026-12-02` extension threshold cut-over and citizenship language test, Portugal's suspended qualified job-search visa, Poland's draft work-permit exemption, Estonia's `2027-01-01` short-term permit abolition, EU Single Permit infringement proceedings, and EU–India FTA ratification), check the [central V0 status table](../visa-and-relocation.md#status-table).

## Nordic countries: high salary floors, clean digital workflows

- **Sweden:** Standard work-permit thresholds jumped from 80% to **90% of national median = SEK 34,470/month** (`2026-06-16`, based on SEK 38,300 median) for first-time applications decided on or after `2026-06-01`, becoming compulsory for extensions registered from **`2026-12-02`** (`T0-40 L51, L104, L137`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A/B]`); students and researchers switching to their first Swedish work permit are exempt from the 90% median floor (`T0-40 L910`, `[B]`). **EU Blue Card** sits at **SEK 53,625/month** (`MIGRFS 2026:11`, `2026-07-15`, exempt from the 90% rule, up to 4 years from `2026-06-01`, `[B · T0-40 L52, L105, L114]`). Fees are SEK 2,200 (adult family SEK 1,500; child SEK 750 — accompanying children aged 16+ carry work rights, `T0-40 L908`, `[A]`). Migrationsverket met its 30-day service promise for >80% of complete highly-skilled applications in 2026 — averaging **21 days** for complete highly-qualified cases (Table 3.18 Category A: 33 days; 2025 overall average 161 days first-time with a 58% overall first-time approval rate, `T0-40 L51–L52, L104, L987–L988`, `[A]`). Citizenship residency extended to **8 years** from `2026-06-06` (language-test deferral pending `2026-09-30`); Sweden's `2026-07-12` permanent-residence removal applies **only to asylum-related permits**, not work permits (`T0-40 L52, L117–L118, L158`, `[A]`).
- **Denmark:** Denmark opts out of the EU Blue Card (`T0-40 L53`, `[D]`). Employers instead use the **Pay Limit Scheme** (**DKK 552,000/yr** from `2026-01-01`, up from 514,000 in 2025; fee DKK 6,810; 1–3 months, or 1–2 months with ~10-day quick start under SIRI Fast-Track) or the **Supplementary Pay Limit** (**DKK 446,000/yr**, 2–4 months) (`T0-40 L53, L175–L183`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A]`). Qualifying pay must reflect guaranteed cash salary, pension, and paid holiday on at least **30 hours/week**; extensions are **grandfathered** at the initial year's salary floor, and permits include an automatic **6-month job-seeking permit** upon involuntary job loss (`T0-40 L911, L960`, `[A]`). A collective-agreement track at DKK 322,000 (2026 base) is enacted for `2027-01-01` (`T0-40 L53, L184`, `[B]`). Denmark's Positive List (higher education, refreshed quarterly on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July, and 1 October; standard 3-year IT bachelor's gate) includes IT Engineer, Software Backend Developer, and Chief Data Officer, but **no AI, ML, or data-scientist title** in the verified extract (`T0-40 L53, L185–L189, L911`, `[A]`). Permanent residence takes **8 years** (or **4 years** meeting four supplementary criteria including DKK 346,155.57 income and Danish 3; fee DKK 7,570, 12 months) (`T0-40 L53`, `[A]`).
- **Finland:** Specialist and EU Blue Card permits share a **€3,937/month** 2026 floor (up from €3,827 in 2025; EU Blue Card requires ≥6 months confirmed employment), with a **2-week fast-track target** for first-time applicants (and accompanying family) applying from abroad and a €750 online fee (`2026-01-01`) (`T0-40 L54, L232, L915`, `[A]`). Startup and graduate routes apply regional net-income floors (**€1,210/month** Helsinki, **€1,090** other large cities, **€1,030** elsewhere, `T0-40 L917–L918`, `[A]`). Permanent residence (fee **€380** online / **€600** paper, `T0-40 L966, L1000`, `[A]`) tightened on `2026-01-08` to **6 years** (+2y work + language), but high-earning tech workers retain a **4-year fast track** via >€40,000/year income or a master's/PhD plus 2 years' work, and **graduates of Finnish university Bachelor's or Master's degrees have no minimum residence-year wait for PR** upon meeting language and employment conditions (`T0-40 L54, L243, L270, L917, L967`, `[A]`).
- **Norway:** Non-EU skilled workers (`faglært`) require **NOK 599,200/yr** (Master's) or **NOK 522,600/yr** (Bachelor's) where no collective agreement applies (`2025-09-01`) (`T0-40 L55, L287`, `[A]`). Permanent residence requires oral A2 Norwegian plus a social-studies test (`2025-09-01`) (`T0-40 L55`, `[A]`). Between January and October 2026, Indians received 829 of 9,638 Norwegian work permits (353 of 2,962 skilled permits = 11.9%, fourth overall) (`T0-40 L55, L319–L320`, `[A]`).

## Southern & Central Europe: lower salary thresholds and tax regimes

- **Spain:** Combines one of Europe's fastest statutory clocks (**20 business days** via UGE) with moderate floors under `Order PJC/44/2026` (**€41,356.36/yr** general EU Blue Card / national highly-qualified; **€33,085.09/yr** reduced for hard-to-fill roles or recent ≤3-year graduates) (`T0-40 L56, L336–L337`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A]`). The **Teleworker / Digital Nomad Visa** (Law 28/2022) requires 200% of Spain's SMI (**~€2,849/month** in 2026, live check `[B]`) for foreign-employer remote work (≤20% Spanish-client revenue), and incoming workers may elect Spain's special incoming-worker tax regime (24% flat withholding up to €600,000 for move year + 5 years) (`T0-40 L56, L64, L338`, `[A/B]`).
- **Portugal:** Law 61/2025 (`2025-10-23`) repealed the expression-of-interest path and replaced the open job-search visa with a restricted qualified job-search visa (carrying a **1-year re-application bar** on unused visas, `T0-40 L925`, `[B]`) — which remains **effectively suspended** as of `2026-11-19` because the implementing *portaria* defining eligible professions has not been published (`T0-40 L57, L397, L414`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Law 62/2026 (`2026-09-11`) allows employer changes via AIMA notification (`T0-40 L57, L398`, `[B]`). Tech Visa requires an IAPMEI-accredited firm plus Bachelor's + 5 years' experience (highly-qualified residence visas carry a 120-day residence-appointment scheduling window, `T0-40 L57, L433, L923`, `[A/B]`); IFICI offers a 20% tax rate for qualifying scientific/tech roles (`T0-40 L64, L399`, `[B]`).
- **Poland:** EU Blue Card floor stands at **PLN 13,355.34/month** (150% of the 2025 national average PLN 8,903.56; card PLN 100 + stamp PLN 440; up to 3 years; 5,853 Blue Cards issued in 2024) (`T0-40 L58, L441, L448, L475`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A/B]`). Standard employer work-permit fees are **PLN 200** (≤3 months) or **PLN 400** (>3 months; secondary summary, `T0-40 L927`, `[C]`). Work-permit filings went online-only on `2026-03-04`; a `2026-07-10` draft exemption for select highly-qualified categories remains pending (`T0-40 L58`, `[B]`).
- **Estonia:** Official salary table (`2026-03-05` to March 2027) sets 1.0× national average at **€2,092/month**, 0.8× shortage at €1,674, 1.24× at €2,594, and **1.5× top specialist at €3,138/month** (exempt from the ~1,292–1,300 annual quota) (`T0-40 L61, L491, L520`, `[A/B]`). Aliens Act reforms phased in across three dates: 6-month real-economic-activity sponsor rule (`2026-01-01`), 30-day employer-change flexibility (`2026-05-22`), and abolition of short-term permit categories (`2027-01-01`) (`T0-40 L61`; dates reconciled via live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`).
- **Austria:** EU Blue Card floor is **€55,678/yr** + special payments (2026; 24 months, €218); Red-White-Red (RWR) Other Key Workers is **€3,465/mo** + 55/90 points + labour-market test; **RWR Shortage Occupations** (55 points across 64 federal shortage occupations, narrowed from 81 in 2026) carries **no general statutory salary floor** beyond the applicable collective agreement; and **RWR Very Highly Qualified** (≥70 points) grants a **6-month job-search visa D** — the only verified no-offer job-search visa across these ten countries (`T0-40 L59, L540–L543, L560–L562, L929–L931, L950`, `[A/B]`). Spouses of RWR and Blue Card holders receive an **RWR Plus** card with unrestricted labour-market access (`T0-40 L929–L931`, `[B]`).
- **Italy:** EU Blue Card sits outside Italy's 2026–2028 *decreto flussi* quotas (497,550 total), uses an approximate **~€36,300/yr** ISTAT average floor, accepts 3 years in 7 for ICT specialists, and grants **immediate family reunification regardless of contract duration** (`T0-40 L60, L592, L933`, `[A]`). Expect portal bottlenecks and conflicting 30-day vs 90-day `nulla osta` timelines (`T0-40 L60, L611`, `[B]`). Italy's impatriate tax regime (50% exemption up to €600,000) explicitly covers remote work for a foreign employer under a `2026-01-12` tax ruling (`T0-40 L60, L64, L601`, `[B]`); note that Italy's Digital Nomad Visa **bars work for Italian-registered entities** (secondary summaries, `T0-40 L934`, `[C]`).

## EU-level developments and Indian-national specifics

- **EU Blue Card share:** Eurostat 2024 data shows Indian citizens received **16,268 of 78,096 EU Blue Cards (20.8%)** plus 3,346 ICT permits across the bloc (`T0-40 L63, L678`, `[A]`).
- **EU directives and Talent Pool:** The **EU Talent Pool** (`Reg. (EU) 2026/1047`) entered into force `2026-06-01`, but the operational matching platform is not expected until late 2027 (`T0-40 L62, L643`, `[A]`). On `2026-07-15`, the European Commission opened infringement proceedings against 17 member states (including AT, FI, PL, PT, SE — as well as DE, FR, NL — but not DK, EE, IT, ES) for late transposition of the **Single Permit Recast** (`Dir. 2024/1233`, deadline `2026-05-21`) (`T0-40 L62, L641`, `[A]`).
- **India mobility, attestation & EPFO social-security split:** The **EU–India Free Trade Agreement** concluded negotiations on `2026-01-27` alongside an EU–India Mobility MoU, but signature and ratification remain pending (`T0-40 L63`, `[A/B]`). All ten countries accept **Hague Apostilles** from India (`T0-40 L63`, `[A]`). Bilateral mobility agreements (MEA list; texts unread) cover AT, DK, FI (`2026-03-05`), IT, and PT. **EPFO bilateral Social-Security Agreements (SSAs)** — allowing seconded workers to obtain a Certificate of Coverage and avoid double social-security contributions — are in force with **six** of these countries (**AT, DK, FI, NO, PT, SE**) and **absent for four** (**EE, IT, PL, ES**) (`T0-40 L63, L983`, `[A]`).

## Decision rules by profile

- **Mid-level engineer with an offer:** **Finland** (2-week fast track, €3,937/mo, 4-year PR at >€40k/yr), **Spain** (20-business-day Blue Card at €41,356 + 24% incoming-worker tax), and **Estonia** (€3,138/mo quota-exempt top specialist) offer the lowest friction; **Sweden** and **Denmark** suit higher-salary offers clearing SEK 34,470/mo (or Blue Card SEK 53,625) and DKK 552,000/yr (`T0-40 L51–L61`).
- **Remote contractor or foreign-employer employee seeking an EU base:** **Spain's Teleworker (DNV)** (~€2,849/mo, 200% SMI, 24% incoming-worker tax) is the cleanest verified statutory DNV in this group (`T0-40 L56, L64, L338`); **Portugal D8** and **Italy DNV** rely more heavily on unverified vendor summaries and face longer administrative queues (`T0-40 L64`).
- **Senior candidate without an offer:** Only **Austria's RWR Very Highly Qualified** (≥70 points → 6-month search visa D) provides a verified job-search route across these ten countries; Portugal's qualified job-search visa remains unusable pending its implementing *portaria* (`T0-40 L57, L59, L414, L562`).

## Evidence and limits

- **Retrieval coverage (`T0-40`):** 54 of 128 sources were read directly (`migrationsverket.se`, `nyidanmark.dk`, `migri.fi`, `udi.no`, `boe.es`, `migration.gv.at`, `politsei.ee`), while Portugal's AIMA portal and several DNV guidance pages blocked fetches (`T0-40 L4`), capping Portuguese D8 and Italian DNV figures at Grade `[C]`.
- **Absences:** No country in this set publishes GenAI-specific permit counts; Denmark's Positive List and most national shortage lists omit explicit "AI/ML engineer" titles; bilateral mobility agreement texts were not opened in primary form.
