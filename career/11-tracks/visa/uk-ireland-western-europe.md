# Visa Dossier: UK, Ireland & Western Europe

> [!CAUTION]
> **Pointers to official sources, not legal or immigration advice.** Thresholds in Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland, and Switzerland adjust annually around January–March, and UK settlement rules are awaiting an implementing Statement of Changes. Verify every rule on the national portal (`gov.uk`, `enterprise.gov.ie`, `make-it-in-germany.com`, `ind.nl`, `service-public.fr`, `sem.admin.ch`) and with local counsel before acting.

For contested and pending rules across these six countries (UK Earned Settlement Statement of Changes, Germany 2027 Blue Card Bundesrat adoption, Netherlands Belastingplan 2027 Senate vote and 2027 IND norms, France contract-length conflict, and Switzerland 2027 federal quotas), check the [central V0 status table](../visa-and-relocation.md#status-table).

## United Kingdom

- **Skilled Worker (RQF 6 floor):** Requires the higher of **£41,700/yr** or the occupation's Table 1 going rate — for example, SOC 2134 (programmers and software developers) **£54,700** (lower rate £40,000 for qualifying new entrants/transitionals), SOC 2133 (data architects/engineers) **£54,900**, SOC 2139 (DevOps/cyber) **£52,300**, and SOC 2433 (statistical data scientists) **£55,100** (`T0-39 L72, L95, L186`, `[A]`). There is **no AI-specific SOC code**, and SOC 3544 (data analysts, Table 1a RQF 3–5) is **ineligible** for new sponsorship (`T0-39 L72, L192`, `[A]`). From `2026-04-08` (HC 1691), salary compliance is checked **per pay period**, and fees rose to **£819** (≤3y) / **£1,618** (>3y) outside the UK, **£943** / **£1,865** inside, sponsor licence £611 small / £1,682 large, Certificate of Sponsorship £525, plus Immigration Health Surcharge **£1,035/yr** (`T0-39 L72, L161, L220–L223`, `[B]`).
- **Global Talent (Digital Technology & Research):** Unsponsored, £561 endorsement + £205 visa = **£766** (+ IHS £1,035/yr) (`T0-39 L74, L162, L239`, `[A]`). GOV.UK updated endorsement waits on `2026-10-27` to **within 3 weeks outside the UK / 8 weeks inside** (`T0-39 L74, L958`, `[A]`, replacing undated "5–8 weeks"). Although the GOV.UK apply page omits the name, live verification (`2026-11-19`, `[B]`) confirms **Tech Nation** (under Founders Forum Group) continues under Home Office contract as the official Digital Technology endorsing body (Exceptional Talent & Exceptional Promise). Academic/research routes via Royal Society, RAEng, and UKRI run 2 weeks (fast-track) or 5 weeks (peer review) (`T0-39 L74, L162`, `[A]`).
- **Earned Settlement and B2 English:** The Home Office response *A Fairer Pathway to Settlement* (CP 1707, published `2026-11-02`) sets a **10-year default**, a **5-year route at ≥£50,271** (or qualifying public service), and a **3-year route at ≥£125,141** (closing Long Residence and excluding Graduate/GBM time); no implementing Statement of Changes has been laid as of `2026-11-19` (`T0-39 L71, L156, L206, L961`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[A/B]`). **B2 English for settlement** is already enacted under HC 1691 effective **`2027-03-26`** (including Global Talent `GT 13.1(b)`) (`T0-39 L175, L233`, `[A/B]`).
- **Graduate & HPI routes:** The Graduate visa shrinks from 2 years (applications ≤`2026-12-31`) to **18 months** from **`2027-01-01`** (3 years PhD; £937 + IHS; does not count toward settlement) (`T0-39 L73, L165`, `[A]`). The High Potential Individual (HPI) Global Universities List (`2026-11-01`, 82 universities) contains **zero Indian institutions** (`T0-39 L137, L190, L241`, `[A]`).
- **India-specific UK provisions:** Youth Mobility / Young Professionals Scheme (YPS) offers 3,000 places/yr (ages 18–30, £340; 2026 ballots closed) (`T0-39 L167, L285`, `[A]`). The **UK–India CETA and Double Contribution Convention** (up to 60-month social-security detachment for seconded employees) entered into force `2026-07-15`, and GBM Service Supplier provisions for Indians took effect `2026-04-08` (`T0-39 L171, L248, L274–L275`, `[B]`). Indian applicants need a TB certificate valid 6 months (`T0-39 L285`, `[A]`).

## Ireland

- **Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP):** From `2026-03-01`, floors rose to **€40,904/yr** (occupation on the Critical Skills Occupations List + relevant degree), **€68,911/yr** (non-CSOL/without relevant degree tier — wording varies slightly across DETE pages), and **€36,848/yr** (graduates within 12 months); €1,000 application fee; 2-year job offer required (`T0-39 L81, L345, L373`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[A]`).
- **Occupation-list nuance:** The updated CSOL (`S.I. 213 of 2026`, effective `2026-05-13`) explicitly includes SOC 2136 (programmers and software developers), 2137, and 2139, but **does not name AI engineer, ML engineer, or data scientist** — employers must map roles to SOC 2136/2139 or pay the €68,911 tier (`T0-39 L81, L375–L376`, `[A]`).
- **Fastest open-market transition in Europe:** CSEP holders qualify for unrestricted **Stamp 4** after **21 months** from permit commencement (updated `2026-02-25`, replacing the prior 2-year rule) and receive immediate spousal open work rights (`T0-39 L81–L82, L345, L378`, `[A]`).
- **General Employment Permit (GEP) & ICT:** GEP requires €36,605 (€34,009 Irish Level 8+ grad), a Labour Market Needs Test, and a 50:50 EEA staffing rule; ICT/Contract for Services sits at €49,523 (`T0-39 L81, L346, L373`, `[A/B]`). On `2026-11-12`, DETE queues trailed by ~3 weeks (CSEP) and ~4 weeks (GEP); New Delhi Embassy visa processing runs 4–6 weeks (Category A) or 8–10 weeks (Category B) (`T0-39 L82, L406–L409`, `[A/B]`).

## Germany

- **EU Blue Card (2026 binding floors; 2027 pending):** The binding 2026 floors (BMI `BAnz AT 18.12.2025 B3`) are **€50,700/yr** standard and **€45,934.20/yr** reduced (45.3% of the €101,400 pension ceiling) for shortage occupations including IT/STEM (`T0-39 L75, L508`, `[A]`). Draft 2027 social-security rules (`Drucksache 576/26`, €106,200 ceiling) imply **€53,100** / **€48,108.60**, pending Bundesrat approval and December BMI publication (`T0-39 L75, L544–L545, L963`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[M]`). Applicants aged 45+ on non-Blue-Card skilled permits face a €55,770 floor (`T0-39 L508`, `[A]`).
- **Two IT-without-degree routes (updating `T0-08`):** Germany's January 2026 federal rules provide **two** degree-free IT paths, both requiring Federal Employment Agency (BA) approval: (1) **Blue Card IT route** with 3 years' experience in the last 7 at **€45,934.20/yr**, and (2) **IT specialist route** with **2 years' experience in the last 5 at €45,630/yr** (`T0-39 L76, L512, L534, L595, L962`, `[A]`).
- **Fast-track (§81a) & Opportunity Card (§20a):** Prior Phase 0 guidance (`T0-08`) cited "2–3 months end-to-end" for §81a; no official end-to-end duration is published — Munich publishes only component clocks (2–4 weeks fast-track steps + up to 3 weeks visa decision; fee up to €411 + €75 visa) (`T0-39 L77, L962`, `[A]`). The **Opportunity Card** (Chancenkarte) allows a 1-year no-offer job search with 6 points + A1 German or B2 English + **€1,091 net/month** blocked funds and 20 hours/week trial/part-time work (`T0-39 L76, L514`, `[A]`).
- **Settlement & India specifics:** Blue Card permanent settlement runs **27 months** (A1 German) or **21 months** (B1 German) (`T0-39 L77`, `[A]`). Indians received over 25% of German Blue Cards in 2023; note that **Germany objects to Indian Hague Apostilles** — New Delhi rolled out digitised `Urkundenüberprüfung` from March 2026 (€400/€500, ~8 weeks; official leaflet states no police clearance required) (`T0-39 L557`, `[A]`). Media reports of a "90,000 Indian visa quota" remain unconfirmed by federal statute (`T0-39 L557`, `[C]`).

## Netherlands

- **2026 HSM and EU Blue Card norms (all fetch-summary `[B]`):** Effective `2026-01-01` (gross monthly, **excluding 8% holiday allowance**): Highly Skilled Migrant (HSM) **€5,942/mo** (age ≥30), **€4,357/mo** (<30), **€3,122/mo** (reduced / zoekjaar); EU Blue Card **€5,942/mo** standard, **€4,754/mo** reduced (3y ICT experience in 7y accepted; no recognised sponsor required; 45–90 days) (`T0-39 L78, L644–L645, L662`, `[B]`). HSM requires an IND-recognised sponsor (up to 3 months for new sponsor recognition; IND aims for 2 weeks on complete HSM filings) (`T0-39 L78`, `[B]`). Binding **2027 IND salary norms** remain unpublished as of `2026-11-19` (historically released late December; live check `[B]`).
- **30% → 27% expat tax ruling:** Legislation reduces the maximum tax-free allowance from 30% to **27%** starting **`2027-01-01`** for employees entering the scheme in 2024 or later (pre-2024 users keep 30% under transitional rules; 2025+ entrants also face higher indexed tax-ruling salary floors, separate from IND immigration thresholds — €46,107 in 2026 / €50,436 cited for 2027, €38,388 for <30 master's) (`T0-39 L79, L664`, `[B]`). The accompanying *Pakket Belastingplan 2027* passed the Tweede Kamer (76–74) on `2026-11-12` and moved to the Eerste Kamer for late November/December debate (live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`).
- **Zoekjaar, naturalisation, and India MVV:** The 1-year open **Zoekjaar** orientation permit covers graduates of top-200 universities in at least two of THE, QS, or ShanghaiRanking (ARWU or GRAS by subject) with Nuffic valuation and IELTS 6.0 (`T0-39 L80, L635`, `[B]`). A proposal to extend naturalisation from 5 to 10 years remains a draft memo only (`T0-39 L79`, `[B]`). Indian nationals collect the MVV entry sticker in New Delhi, Mumbai, or Bengaluru within 3 months of IND approval (≤10 working days processing + ≤10 return; DTDC INR 850) (`T0-39 L80, L672, L691`, `[B]`).

## France

- **All France figures are capped at Grade `[B]`** because `legifrance.gouv.fr` and `france-visas.gouv.fr` blocked automated retrieval (`T0-39 L83`).
- **EU Blue Card & Decree 2026-1001 (`2026-10-30`):** The EU Blue Card floor rose to **€59,373/yr** (1.5× the €39,582 reference wage, effective `2025-08-31`, replacing €53,836.50) with a 3-year degree or 5 years' experience (3 years in 7 for listed ICT roles) (`T0-39 L83, L768, L964`, `[B]`). **Decree 2026-1001** (`2026-10-26`, effective `2026-10-30`) sets a **90-day** decision deadline (30 days intra-EU Blue Card), grants up to a 4-year card for ≥2-year contracts (contract + 3 months, max 2y for shorter contracts), cuts the employer-change lock-in from **12 to 6 months**, and adds explicit 3–6 month unemployment search windows (`T0-39 L83, L751, L786–L787`; confirmed live `2026-11-19`, `[B]`). Minimum contract duration remains contested across French official pages (`service-public.fr` `2026-11-06` lists no minimum, conflicting with older 1-year France-Visas text and 6-month April 2025 statute, `T0-39 L83, L768`, `[B]`).
- **Other Talent Passport tracks & 2026 language exam:** `Talent — salarié qualifié` requires a French Master 2 (or qualifying innovative-company role) at **€39,582/yr**; `chercheur` €2,200 + host agreement (60d); `porteur de projet` SMIC €21,876.40 + €30,000 financing; `mandataire social` €65,629.20; fee €350 on grant; `talent-famille` included (`T0-39 L83, L964`, `[B]`). Effective `2026-01-01`, multi-year cards require **A2 French**, 10-year `carte de résident` requires **B1**, naturalisation B2, plus a mandatory civic exam (whether Talent cards are exempt from A2 remains contested, `T0-39 L750, L777, L793`, `[B]`).

## Switzerland

- **Tight non-EU quotas:** For 2026, the Swiss Federal Council set third-country quotas at **8,500 total = 4,500 B + 4,000 L** (reversing the earlier 4,000 B / 4,500 L split), alongside separate 3,500 UK and 3,500 EU/EFTA >120-day service-provider quotas (`T0-39 L84, L887, L900`, `[A]`). As of `2026-11-19`, 2027 quota decrees have not been confirmed published (live check `[B]`).
- **High employer bar:** Employers must pass a strict domestic and EU/EFTA priority-recruitment test, respect cantonal salary norms, and comply with `Stellenmeldepflicht` (5% unemployment threshold) (`T0-39 L84, L887`, `[A]`).
- **2026 political & bilateral milestones:** Voters **rejected** the *"No to 10 million Switzerland"* initiative on `2026-06-14` (`T0-39 L889`, `[A/B]`). The **EFTA–India TEPA** entered into force `2025-10-01` (SECO `[A]`), though Annex 8A mobility commitments (reported by secondary commentary as visas for independent professionals and ICT spouses) were not verified in primary treaty text (`T0-39 L892, L1287`, `[B]`).

## Decision rules by profile

- **Mid-level engineer with an offer:** **Ireland (CSEP → 21-month Stamp 4)**, **Germany (Blue Card → 21–27m PR)**, and **Netherlands (HSM 2-week IND target)** offer the cleanest sponsor predictability (`T0-39 L75–L82`). In the UK, verify the offer clears both the SOC going rate (typically £52k–£55k+) and the £50,271 5-year Earned Settlement threshold (`T0-39 L71–L72`).
- **Senior practitioner without an offer:** Target **UK Global Talent (Digital Tech)** if your evidence fits Tech Nation's criteria (3-week offshore endorsement), **Germany's Opportunity Card** (1-year search), or **Netherlands Zoekjaar** if holding a top-200 degree within 3 years (`T0-39 L74, L76, L80`).
- **Switzerland-bound candidate:** Treat Switzerland as an employer-initiated multinational transfer or niche senior research offer — cold applications from India rarely clear cantonal priority tests and the 8,500 non-EU cap (`T0-39 L84, L887`).

## Pitfalls and vendor myths

- **Citing "72%" or "54–65%" UK Global Talent pass rates:** Prior Phase 0 notes (`T0-08`) cited these rates, but `T0-39` traced them to **no primary or published source** (`[E]` — dropped); the only figure surfaced (63% in 2024) came from a commercial agency without denominators or source links (`T0-39 L74, L959`, `[C/E]`).
- **Assuming UK HPI covers IITs:** The `2026-11-01` HPI Global Universities List (82 institutions) includes **no Indian university** (`T0-39 L190, L241`, `[A]`).
- **Submitting an Indian Hague Apostille to Germany:** Germany objected to India's apostille accession; plan ~8 weeks for New Delhi's digitised `Urkundenüberprüfung` instead (`T0-39 L557`, `[A]`).
- **Confusing Dutch IND HSM thresholds with the 27% tax-ruling floor:** They are set under separate statutes; clearing IND's HSM norm does not automatically qualify an employee for the 27% expat ruling (`T0-39 L662–L664`; live check `2026-11-19`, `[B]`).

## Evidence and limits

- **Retrieval coverage (`T0-39`):** 99 of 155 sources were read directly (`gov.uk`, `enterprise.gov.ie`, `make-it-in-germany.com`, `sem.admin.ch`), while Netherlands and France relied on fetch summaries and blocked portals (`legifrance.gouv.fr`, `france-visas.gouv.fr`), capping NL and FR figures at Grade `[B]`.
- **Absences:** No national register splits GenAI roles; UK Earned Settlement implementing Rules, Germany's binding 2027 BMI Blue Card notice, Netherlands' 2027 IND norms, and Switzerland's 2027 quota decree were all pending as of `2026-11-19`.
