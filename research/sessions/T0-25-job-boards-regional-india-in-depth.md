S-numbers refer to rows in the Sources & Evidence Ledger (S1 to S118). Grades follow the protocol (A primary, B established secondary or platform data, C attributed third-party, D memory or anecdote, M my own calculation, P practice). Research date is 2026-11-11. Counts are what public pages showed without login; I created no accounts and bypassed no access controls. LinkedIn refused automated access (Blocked). ## Research Question

**Decision.** Which national and regional boards carry GenAI roles in each market a GenAI professional might target, with India measured in depth, and which are worth a job seeker's time (D-005, D-009; `channels-and-boards.md`, `india-remote-playbook.md`, `tools.json`).

**Reading applied.** "National board" means a board whose inventory or audience is one country or language area, including government boards. For the US and Canada I took Dice, USAJOBS and Job Bank (not Indeed, LinkedIn or Glassdoor). Cross-border boards, ghost-job law, pay transparency and scams belong to the global session; scam evidence is noted only where it affects a board verdict. Israel is grouped with the Gulf as "Middle East" for routines.

**Method.**
- About 70 searches and fetches (standard and extended modes) in English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Hebrew, Korean, and Traditional and Simplified Chinese.
- India: direct observation of foundit's public GenAI search page (2026-11-11). Public posting pages from foundit, Hirist and Cutshort were classified by employer type. An AmbitionBox page that mirrors Naukri-sourced listings was used as a small sample.
- Samples: foundit n=36 postings (20 first-page plus 16 detail pages); Hirist n=18 distinct employers; Cutshort n=17 employers (18 postings); LinkedIn n≈16 from public snippets; AmbitionBox/Naukri-sourced n=6 named employers. All are non-random samples drawn from pages the search index exposed, and the LinkedIn and AmbitionBox samples are below the 30-posting threshold.
- Falsification: compared JobsPipe's India coverage with Indian boards' own counts; read foundit's first page for false positives; compared AmbitionBox counts with Naukri counts; tested whether Make it in Germany's IT feed surfaces GenAI roles; searched for independent evidence that Naukri's paid tiers raise response rates (none found); searched for contrary reports on the 74% recruiter figure (none found).
- Employer type is classified by business, not legal entity. GCCs often post under the parent brand, so "no identifiable GCC" is a floor, not a finding of absence.

## Recommendation

| # | Decision rule | Failure mode | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | India, 3+ years: run LinkedIn plus Naukri (free tier) plus one specialist board (Hirist for services and analytics employers; Cutshort or Wellfound for startups). ## Open Questions & Risks

- Owner-run logged-in counts with fixed queries, and a 30-posting employer-type sample per board, would settle the Naukri and LinkedIn questions.
- Duplicate rates across boards (Info Edge mirrors, staffing re-posts) are unmeasured.
- Third-party snapshots may predate the dates given; several are undated.
- Search-engine location may influence what pages appear.
- Counts expire within 31 days.

## Discovered Concerns

1. foundit matches on job text: a posting titled "SEO Project Manager (Must Write Content Without Generative AI)" appears in GenAI results [S13].
2. AmbitionBox lists only a small subset of Naukri-sourced postings (Bengaluru 87 against 4,007 on Naukri); do not use its counts as a Naukri proxy [S95].
3. Naukrigulf paid-service complaint (USD 472 "refundable" fee) is a scam signal; route to the global session [S36].
4. Entry-level squeeze signals recur across markets and bear on fresher strategy: France (CDI hires of 21–29 year-olds fell 28.3% in GenAI-exposed occupations since Nov 2022 against 7.5% in the least exposed, Banque de France and HEC note) [S58]; Spain (tech vacancies needing no experience fell 41% in 2025) [S108]; Ireland (almost half of employers cut entry and graduate roles) [S97].
5. A Cutshort posting with inconsistent seniority and pay is a quality signal [S26].
6. JobsPipe's India sample is consulting-heavy and should not be used as a market-size source [S1].
7. A JobsDB Hong Kong ad offering HK$400–900 an hour for a "paid trial task" under NDA is a quality caution; route to the global session [S117].
8. National-market comparisons here lean on cross-border operators' own data (Indeed, SEEK) that carries commercial interest [S102], [S85].
