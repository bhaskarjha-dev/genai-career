## Research Question

**Decision question.** For job seekers in every role family, in particular India-based readers whose employers are IT-services firms, global capability centres (GCCs) and hyperscaler or platform partners, which GenAI-relevant certifications do Databricks, Snowflake, Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Palantir, UiPath, MongoDB, Elastic, Dataiku and DataRobot offer as of 2026-11-13; what does each involve and cost; how much hiring signal and channel value does each carry; and in what order is each worth taking, if at all?

**Sub-questions.** (1) Census of credentials with exact names, codes, formats, fees, validity and status. (2) Seed verification: confirm each seed with its exact name or report an absence. (3) Posting-level signal in the US and India. (4) Partner-programme and employer-channel value. (5) Rigour and verifiability. (6) Skill alignment to role families. (7) Cost to earn, including India. (8) Currency and reorganisation since 2025-01-01. (9) Provisional order per role family.

**Scope as applied.** Proctored exams are kept apart from course-completion certificates, accreditations and skill badges; the credential table says which is which. General credentials (Databricks Data Engineer, Snowflake SnowPro Core, Salesforce Data 360 Consultant) appear only as the probes name them or as pairings seen in postings. Reading choices where the brief was ambiguous: "Databricks Context Engineer" is resolved to the exact official name on the issuer page; "SAP Business AI" is resolved to the SAP exam whose title contains it (a presales/positioning exam, not a developer exam); "ServiceNow certifications for Now Assist and AI agents" is resolved to micro-certifications and delivery accreditations because no proctored stand-alone exam was found; "Salesforce Agentforce Sales/Service Consultant" is treated as a rename of existing consultant exams (C sources), not a GenAI exam. Credentials from other groups are mentioned only as overlaps.

**Method.** (a) Issuer pages and exam guides opened where the fetch tool allowed (grade A); where only a search extract was available the row says Extract and is graded B at most. (b) Partner-programme pages and announcements from the issuers. (c) My own posting sample: 14 queries run with the session's web_search tool in standard mode on 2026-11-13, one results page per query (17–20 results), read at snippet level; the exact queries are in ledger rows S94–S107. A result counts as a posting if it is a single job listing; the same employer, title and text on several mirrors is merged by hand into one posting (n). "Names the credential" means the visible text names it (including a former name) as required, preferred or an example; "required" means the text says required, mandatory or must-have. US and India are separated by query keywords and by the location shown. (d) Falsification searches: I searched for postings naming each credential by name (a design that favours finding hits) and still recorded zero or near-zero counts for the Databricks GenAI Engineer Associate (US), the Snowflake Gen AI Specialty, MongoDB, DataRobot and the Elastic GenAI Associate; I also searched for a ServiceNow stand-alone AI exam and for MongoDB or Elastic proctored GenAI exams and found none.

**Limits of the sample (stated once).** Each query contains the credential name, so counts show how often a credential appears among postings that match it, not its share of all GenAI postings. Snippets show the passage matching the query, so a posting that names a credential elsewhere can be missed. Samples are small (n = 9 to 17 per cell), not random, and several results are the vendor's own postings. The brief's 10% test therefore cannot be run from this sample; Detailed Findings gives a reproducible audit design.

## Recommendation

Decision rules for a job seeker, in order:

1. **Treat these as stack credentials, not general GenAI credentials.** Take one only when a named employer, client or partner channel you target runs that platform. Without such a target, none of the 54 credentials in the census is indicated by the evidence here. *Failure mode:* a platform is chosen for its brand and the target employer uses another.
2. **Salesforce ecosystem roles (Agentforce developer, consultant, forward-deployed engineer; India services and GCC channels especially):** take the Agentforce Specialist first (USD 200, USD 100 retake), then the Data 360 Consultant if postings pair them (they did in several). Agentblazer Status is free and can come first. *Failure mode:* exam parameters here are secondary-sourced, and Salesforce retired one AI credential and renamed another within about 14 months.
3. **Databricks:** choose by role. ML or MLOps: Machine Learning Professional (the Associate first if you lack MLflow practice). Data pipelines for AI: Data Engineer Associate then Professional. GenAI applications on Databricks: Generative AI Engineer Associate (tier Optional, as the knowledge base holds). Context Engineer Associate: watch; take it only after hands-on work with Genie, Lakebase and MCP on a team that runs Databricks. *Failure mode:* version churn (four catalogue events in 2026) and near-zero US posting signal.
4. **Snowflake:** SnowPro Core before any specialty or advanced exam; Gen AI Specialty (USD 225) only for a Snowflake partner or a Cortex-based team; the Advanced MLOps Engineer exam is a beta (C) to watch. *Failure mode:* exam code and figures changed in May to July 2026; confirm on the official page before paying.
5. **Palantir and ServiceNow:** do not self-fund. Seek access through an employer or partner. For Foundry or AIP roles in contract, defence or public-sector markets, take the Associate exam matching your track, then the USD 350 Specialist exam if postings ask. For ServiceNow, mainline administrator and developer credentials (outside this census) appear in 9 of 10 postings; the AI accreditations are partner-side. *Failure mode:* access codes and fees are not public; plans may fail at registration.
6. **SAP:** if you work on SAP BTP, use the free Joule Studio Developer attempt before 2026-12-31; take Generative AI Developer only if a posting names it and you have confirmed Learning Hub cost. *Failure mode:* opaque pricing, results can take up to 20 business days, and four failed attempts impose a one-year wait.
7. **UiPath:** only for automation roles. The Builder Professional launch discount ends 2026-11-22. *Failure mode:* three agentic exams in 14 months make the credential hard to compare across hiring years.
8. **MongoDB, Elastic, Dataiku, DataRobot:** earn the free badges and accreditations as proof of hands-on work; pay (USD 500 per a C source) for Elastic Certified Engineer only for search-consulting roles. *Failure mode:* badges are unproctored and carry little signal on their own.
9. **Collectors, and when to stop:** (i) free badge on the platform you already use; (ii) the one proctored credential named in at least two of your target postings; (iii) stop at one proctored credential per platform until a posting asks for the next. Never start a credential that is retired, renamed or within a month of a version change without checking the issuer page.
10. **Role families outside Applied AI Engineering, ML Engineering, Forward Deployment and AI Infrastructure** (Research, Evaluation and Safety, Governance, Human Data, Embodied AI, Leadership, Independent): nothing from this group is indicated; see the governance and safety census groups.

*What would change these rules:* a reproducible audit showing a credential in more than 10% of GenAI postings for a market segment; a partner-programme rule that sets a numeric certified-headcount threshold including an AI credential (watch Salesforce, Databricks, Snowflake, ServiceNow, SAP); and any launch, renaming or retirement (see Currency).
