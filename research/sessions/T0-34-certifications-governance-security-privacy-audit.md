## Research Question

**Decision question.** Which GenAI-relevant certifications do the AI governance, security, privacy and audit bodies named in the brief offer as of 2026-11-13, what does each involve and cost, how much hiring signal and channel value does each carry, for which role families, and in what order should a reader take them (decision D-005; knowledge-base files `data/skills_ontology.json`, `credential-roi.md`, `credentials-by-role.md`, `governance-careers.md`)?

**Sub-questions.** (1) Census and seed resolution with exact names, status and terms. (2) Hiring signal from my own samples and published data, for the US, UK, EU and India. (3) Channel value: partner programmes, employer targets, contract clauses, DoD 8140. (4) Rigour, accreditation, verification and exam security. (5) Role-family alignment. (6) Cost to earn and renewal. (7) Currency: launches, renamings, retirements since 2025-01-01. (8) A provisional order per role family. (9) Group probes: ISO/IEC 17024 standing, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and DPDP demand drivers.

**Scope as applied.** Every proctored exam, certificate and badge from the listed issuers that concerns AI governance, security, privacy, audit or AI-related data science, including beta and announced credentials. General credentials (CIPP, CIPM, CIPT, CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP) are in scope because the brief names them. A `kind` distinction separates proctored exams from course-completion certificates. Where the issuer lists a fee in a local currency I give it; fees are list prices in USD otherwise.

**Method.** I opened the issuer pages that the search tool surfaced and that the fetch tool allowed (IAPP handbook and study guide; ISACA AAIA, AAIR, AAISM and AAIG pages and the AAIA candidate guide; CompTIA SecAI+), then used search extracts for the remaining issuer pages. Hiring signal: (a) published mention-rate data from Axial Search (n = 1,997 US AI-governance postings; other segments cited by their n); (b) my own tallies of the GRC Careers board (76 listings on its AI-governance page on 2026-11-12; 24 listings on page 1 of its all-jobs list on 2026-11-13; 8 on its AIGP page); (c) purposive samples of postings surfaced by searches whose queries contained credential words (9 India postings, 7 US, UK and remote postings), which bias counts upwards; (d) targeted searches for postings naming each AI-specific credential. LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri could not be sampled. Disconfirming (falsification) searches: postings naming each new credential; other Axial segments; tender and partner-programme clauses; exam-dump and fraud reports; vendor claims of "required" credentials.

## Recommendation

Decision rules for a job seeker. Evidence for each is in the Key Findings.

1. **Take the domain credential that matches your background before any AI-specific one**: CIPP (E or US) or CIPM for privacy and legal work, CISA for IT audit, CRISC for risk, CISSP or CISM for security. In Axial's US AI-governance sample these are named about as often as AIGP (CISSP 10.4%, CIPP 8.2%, CISM 8.1% against AIGP 8.9%) [KF3, C].
2. **Add AIGP when the target title says AI governance or responsible AI and you lack governance evidence.** It has no prerequisite, lists at USD 649–799 and IAPP advises at least 30 hours of study [KF2, A/B]. Treat it as a tie-breaker: 5 of 76 listings on one governance board carry the tag [KF4, B] and no posting in my samples required it.
3. **Take ISACA's AI credentials only if you already hold a qualifying prerequisite and the employer or client values ISACA**: AAIA, AAISM or AAIR. I found no posting naming any of them, and the Indian Chartered Accountant designation is not on the AAIA or AAIR lists [KF5, KF7, A/C].
4. **For AI security engineering and red teaming, prefer a performance-based credential and a public portfolio** (OffSec OSAI, GIAC GAIPT, GOAA or GAIPS, Hack The Box COAE). Use CompTIA SecAI+ as a low-cost, broad credential for SOC, cloud and GRC staff. No posting in my samples names any of them [KF5, KF9, B/C].
5. **In India, add ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor from a scheme with stated accreditation** if you target IT-services, GCC or consulting readiness roles (five of nine sampled postings name it); add DSCI DCPP, DCPLA or DCDPO only for DPDP privacy roles, because no sampled AI posting named them [KF6, KF10, B/C].
6. **Stop rule.** After one domain credential and one AI-specific credential, further collection has little posting-level support: outside governance and security, no certification exceeds about 3% of postings [KF3]. Spend the next hours on evidence of work.
7. **Do not buy an EU AI Act "certificate" because you believe the Act requires one.** None of the sources I opened says so, and the high-risk dates moved to 2027-12-02 and 2028-08-02 [KF11, B].

**What would change these rules.** A reproducible audit showing more than 10% of GenAI postings in a segment naming one credential (Axial's CISSP at 10.4% in AI governance is the only published figure over the line and is not reproducible); ISC2's standalone AI-security certification (beta hoped for early 2027); AAIG's full launch; DoD 8140 matrix entries for the Data/AI roles; a partner or tender clause naming individual credentials; ISO/IEC 42001 becoming a default RFP clause.

**Failure modes of the recommended options.**

| Option | Failure mode |
|---|---|
| Domain credential first | Mention rate is not a requirement; CISSP and CISA need about five years of experience, so entrants cannot take them yet. |
| AIGP | Tie-breaker only; the blueprint changed in February 2026, so older study material may mislead; no ISO/IEC 17024 listing found for it. |
| ISACA AI credentials | Gated by prerequisites; zero postings found; AAIR's maintenance fee conflicts across ISACA pages; AAIG is still beta. |
| Performance-based AI-security credentials | High cost, several prices vendor-only, zero postings, and the issuers are young (OffSec and GIAC launch dates not all confirmed). |
| ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer or Auditor | Scheme quality varies (some are course certificates); demand evidence is concentrated in services and consulting; no US counts. |
| DSCI credentials | India-only; no regulator or tender recognition found. |

### 9. Demand drivers (group probes)

- **EU AI Act.** Prohibited practices applied from 2025-02-02 and general-purpose AI duties from 2025-08-02 [S50]. The Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, followed a Commission proposal of 2025-11-19, a provisional agreement of 2026-05-07, Parliament approval on 2026-06-16 and Council approval on 2026-06-29; it was published on 2026-07-24 and entered into force on 2026-07-27, moving high-risk obligations to 2027-12-02 (stand-alone Annex III systems) and 2028-08-02 (products) [S50]. The Official Journal text was not opened; law-firm and Council-derived reports agree. EXIN's AICP covers Articles 8, 9 and 10 with ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 23894 and the NIST AI RMF [S36]. A vendor note says ISO/IEC 42001 does not by itself confer a presumption of conformity [S65].
- **ISO/IEC 42001 certification demand.** ISO/IEC 42006:2025 (published 2025-07) sets requirements for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems [S35]. Organisation-level certification is visible among IT-services firms and AI vendors (KF6, KF12). I found no dated count of certificates issued.
- **India's DPDP obligations.** The Rules were notified on 2025-11-13: Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 applied immediately, Rule 4 (consent manager registration) from 2026-11-13 and Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 from 2027-05-13. Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint an India-based DPO answerable to the board and an independent data auditor and run annual impact assessments [S51]. No certification is named in the sources I opened, so DCPP, DCPLA, DCDPO, CIPP/A and CDPSE are skills-aligned rather than mandated.

## Open Questions & Risks

1. **Mention is not requirement.** Axial's rates are proprietary and its pages disagree with themselves; the board's tag method is unstated. My samples confirm the ranking band (CISSP, CISA, CISM, CRISC, CIPP and AIGP each between about 6% and 17% on governance listings) but cannot reproduce Axial's figures.
2. **Launch lag.** AAIR launched 2026-04-15 and GAIPS reached general purchase on 2026-07-28; zero postings may only mean it is too early. The recommendation therefore does not rank new credentials by posting signal.
3. **Conflicts of interest.** IAPP, ISACA, CompTIA, GIAC, PECB and others sell the credentials they describe; the GRC Careers board sells AIGP preparation; Practical DevSecOps sells CAISP and publishes comparisons; training companies sell ISO/IEC 42001 and EU AI Act courses. Their value claims are graded C.
4. **Coverage limits.** Helper agents failed, so coverage is thinner than planned: 14 pages opened in full, most issuer facts are search extracts, and ISACA's CISA, CISM and CRISC details, ISC2's CISSP page and CompTIA's SecAI+ price were not opened. LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri could not be sampled.
5. **India sample.** Nine postings, mostly Bengaluru and IT services, surfaced by credential-laden queries; zeros may reflect truncated extracts. Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune and GCC postings are under-represented.
6. **Price drift.** SecAI+ appears at USD 298–359 across resellers and EUR 276 excluding VAT; AAIR's fee conflicts across ISACA pages; GIAC and CAISP prices are vendor-stated.
7. **Regulatory text.** The Omnibus and DPDP dates rest on law-firm and trade reports rather than the Official Journal and Gazette text. Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules reaches its commencement date on 2026-11-13.

## Discovered Concerns

1. ISACA's AAIR page states an annual maintenance fee of USD 45 / 85 whereas the AAIA and AAISM pages state USD 20 / 35, and the AAISM page says one fee covers all Advanced certificates; the knowledge base should not hard-code a renewal figure for AAIR without checking [S11].
2. A vendor comparison attaches AAIR's launch date and domain names to AAIA, contradicting ISACA's candidate guide; exam-preparation sites repeat one another's errors [S7], [S8].
3. An apparently internal Cloud Security Alliance sales page surfaced in search results; access to it is restricted to CSA staff, so I did not use it.
4. DoD 8140 has Data/AI work roles (AI Risk and Ethics Specialist, AI Test & Evaluation Specialist, AI Adoption Specialist) whose qualification options appear as "coming soon" in GIAC's guide; once populated, they could become a US public-sector channel for governance and security credentials [S52].
5. An IAPP conference pricing page lists an AIGP exam add-on at USD 450 (event-specific; page undated), so attendees of that event may pay less than the list price [S77].
6. PECB separates "certified" (ISO/IEC 17024 schemes) from "certificate holder" (ASTM E2659 certificate programmes); check which applies to the ISO/IEC 42001 credential before treating it as personnel certification [S29].
7. The job board used for part of the sample publishes certification tags and sells AIGP practice material; its counts are useful as a sample frame, not as a neutral measurement [S56]–[S58].
8. The brief's anchor date (2026-11-11) precedes the run by two days; listings dated 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 are included and dated.
