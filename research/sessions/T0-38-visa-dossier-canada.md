## Research Question

**Decision question.** For a GenAI professional who wants to work in Canada, which legal routes exist at the research date, what does each require, cost and take, how likely is an employer to use it for an AI role, and what leads to permanent residence (PR)? Informs D-007 and `career/11-tracks/visa-and-relocation.md`.

**Sub-questions.** (1) Do K1 and K2 hold? (2) Route census: employer-sponsored, points-based, graduate, intra-company, founder, remote-work and provincial routes. (3) Employer use for AI and software roles. (4) Indian-national specifics. (5) Remote work while resident. (6) Status table of rules pending or changed since 2025-01-01. (7) Comparison and three reader recommendations.

**Scope as applied.** Canada only. I read "GenAI professional" as an engineer, researcher or leader whose job maps to NOC 2021 codes 21231 (software engineers and designers), 21211 (data scientists), 21230 and 21232 (developers) or 20012 (computer and information systems managers). NOC has no AI-specific code, so this mapping is my assumption (D). Job-seeker and digital-nomad routes are covered briefly. Tax is a one-line pointer.

## Recommendation

Decision rules for a job seeker. Re-verify each status on its official page within 14 days of acting (D-007).

1. **Mid-level engineer with an offer.** Ask the employer which route it will use. If it can file a GTS LMIA, GTS is the default: ESDC average 9 business days (September 2026) plus a work permit; budget 1-3 months (M). If you have worked at least one continuous full-time year in the previous three for a multinational with a Canadian entity, an intra-company transfer (ICT, code C12) avoids the LMIA and the C$1,000 fee. Failure modes: the employer cannot meet the Category A referral or Labour Market Benefits Plan; it fails the ESDC employer test dated 2026-09-18; the Job Bank wage update of 2026-11-18 lifts the prevailing wage; the India work-permit queue (10 weeks); Quebec needs a CAQ first.
2. **Settlement sequencing.** Once on a skilled permit, build 12 months of skilled Canadian work (CEC minimum), keep language at CLB 7 or above, and keep the Express Entry profile accurate. From 2026-11-03 a role in NOC 21231 or 21211 earns 40 extra points for Canadian experience and 70 for a qualifying offer. Failure modes: draws paused since 2026-10-28; cut-offs (CEC 519 on 2026-10-15) may move after recalculation; a job title that maps to a lower-tier NOC.
3. **Senior engineer or researcher without an offer.** Run two tracks. Track A: an Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker) profile, realistic only with a competitive CRS or French (French rounds cut off at 407-435 in October 2026); no general all-programme round appears in the 2026 round list [S67][S74]. Track B: target employers that file GTS or high-wage LMIAs (positive-LMIA lists show who does). Do not plan around the H-1B pathway or an AI-specific GTS stream until rules are published. Failure modes: no offers from outside Canada; the 2026 round list shows category, French, CEC and provincial rounds but no general round (one vendor states none was held all year) [S67][S74]; the researchers category needs Canadian work experience.
4. **Recent graduate.** From a Canadian institution: PGWP (CLB 7 for degrees), then CEC. From abroad: Federal Skilled Worker needs one year of skilled experience, so the realistic start is GTS Category B with an employer willing to sponsor; French speakers can add Francophone Mobility (C16) outside Quebec. Failure modes: junior sponsorship rates are unknown (Absence); study-permit limits.
5. **Quebec.** Treat as a separate system: CAQ before an LMIA-based permit, PSTQ via Arrima for PR, French weighs heavily; the AI/IT/VFX pilot closed on 2026-01-01. Failure modes: French below the level the selection grid rewards (not verified from an official page); the CAQ step adds time before the federal permit.
6. **Founders.** Do not rely on the Start-up Visa (paused 2026-06-30).
7. **Remote work from Canada.** Visitor remote work is a short stay, not a route; closed permits tie you to the sponsoring employer.

What would change these rules: launch of the H-1B or AI-specific GTS pathway; the 2027 Express Entry category list; the level of rounds after the 2026-11-03 recalculation; the Job Bank wage update of 2026-11-18; the Express Entry overhaul proposed in April 2026; a CEPA mobility chapter; details of the Budget 2026 startup pilot.

### 3. K1 test and the Global Talent Stream probe

ESDC's GTS page (modified 2026-11-10) gives a fee of C$1,000 per position requested, non-refundable and not recoverable from the worker [S1]. "No worker merit test" holds for Category B, where eligibility turns on the occupation list, the Labour Market Benefits Plan and the prevailing wage. It does not hold for Category A, which needs a designated-partner referral, a worker who meets the page's "unique and specialised" test, and a C$80,000 floor for the first two positions per year [S1]. The occupation list is shown as updated December 2022 and includes NOC 21211, 21231, 21230, 21232 and 20012 [S1]; other computing NOCs appear in secondary summaries and should be checked on the page. I found no recruitment-advertising step on the page (not an explicit statement; D).

Employer obligations beyond the fee: a Labour Market Benefits Plan (as I read the page, a mandatory benefit plus at least two complementary benefits; verify wording on S1); the prevailing wage; the employer-eligibility update dated 2026-09-18 on the same page (employer definition; misclassifying workers as independent contractors is prohibited); and a Job Bank wage refresh scheduled for 2026-11-18 [S1].

Timing: "about 1-2 months after an offer" is not an official figure. ## Open Questions & Risks

- **Pause and recalculation.** IRCC said rounds would pause for up to two weeks from 2026-11-03 [S6]; no later round was found by 2026-11-13. Cut-offs after recalculation are unknown; the CEC cut-off of 519 (2026-10-15) was set on the old scoring.
- **Pending launches.** H-1B pathway, AI-specific GTS stream, 2027 categories, Express Entry overhaul and the 2027 worker-transition measure may all land inside any reader's planning window. All are V0.
- **Wage refresh.** The Job Bank update of 2026-11-18 can raise prevailing wages and high-wage thresholds [S1]; its size is unknown.
- **Date discrepancy.** The brief states 2026-11-11 as the research date; retrieval ran on 2026-11-13, and the 2026-11-12 IRCC release [S21] post-dates the brief's anchor.

Strongest objections an expert would raise, with the evidence:

1. *Vendor and consultancy sources dominate the secondary layer.* True for ICT, PGWP, C16, Quebec and Express Entry eligibility. Evidence for: I found stale vendor claims (S50, S62). Evidence against: the load-bearing items (GTS fee, wage floors, Express Entry points, Start-up Visa status, levels plan, processing averages) come from pages I opened (S1-S9).
2. *Processing figures are averages or tool outputs, not individual outcomes.* Agreed. ESDC publishes averages [S2]; IRCC says the two-week goal is probably not met [S3]; the India figure is a summary of IRCC's tool [S23]. No distribution was found.
3. *The employer-use counts come from an aggregator.* The stream totals reconcile (146,531 positions) but the method is undisclosed [S44]; the 2022 comparison uses a different publisher and window [S45]. Treat the 30% ratio as indicative (M).
4. *NOC mapping for GenAI roles is judgemental.* No AI-specific code was found; the same job can be filed under 21231, 21211 or another NOC, and officers decide. This affects the points tier and GTS-list eligibility (D).
5. *Facts may have moved after retrieval.* Eleven rows are V0 or V1; re-verify within 14 days (D-007).
