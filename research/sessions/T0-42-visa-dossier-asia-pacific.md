## Research Question

**Decision.** Which legal work routes exist across eight Asia-Pacific economies (Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia) for a GenAI engineer, researcher or technical leader? What does each require, cost and take, how likely is an employer to use it, and what leads to permanent residence? The answer informs D-007 and `career/11-tracks/visa-and-relocation.md`.

**Reading of the brief.** "GenAI professional" means an engineer, researcher or technical leader with no assumption of an AI-specific visa. Where a route is occupation-listed, I checked whether AI, ML or data titles appear.

**Sub-questions.** Route census; employer reality; Indian nationals; remote work while resident; status table; comparison and three reader recommendations; the K1 test.

**Scope as applied.** Everything in the brief's scope list, with gaps recorded under Absences. Rules are stated as of 2026-11-13, with announced changes and their dates.

**Method.**
- About 50 searches in English, Japanese and Korean, plus 16 page-fetch attempts. Eleven pages were read in full. Five fetches failed: moj.go.kr refused automated access (robots.txt) and I did not work around it; immigration.govt.nz returned a blank page; two Home Affairs URLs and one Gold Card URL were rejected by the fetch tool until a search had surfaced them.
- Chinese-language searches for Taiwan and Hong Kong were not run. That is a gap.
- Disconfirming searches:
  - whether K1's "achievements route excludes technology" is wrong (2022 text listed technology [S93]; the 2026 MOM text does not [S1]);
  - vendor claims that Japanese PR in one year is easy;
  - "salary only" claims for the Taiwan Gold Card;
  - "no job needed" claims for the Hong Kong Top Talent Pass;
  - outdated DE Rantau terms (superseded 2026-12-01);
  - Korea F-1-D stay length (2 vs 3 years);
  - Australia's 2026–27 thresholds (two sources disagree with the majority).
- No individual approval odds are estimated.

## Recommendation

**Decision rules.**
- **R1. Offer in hand.** Ask the employer to run the route's own check before you resign or relocate:
  - Singapore: MOM's Self-Assessment Tool.
  - Australia: stream threshold and occupation list.
  - Japan: Engineer/Specialist vs HSP points.
  - Korea: E-7 wage floor.
  - New Zealand: accreditation and job check.
  - Malaysia: category by basic salary.
  - Failure modes: Singapore COMPASS fails if your nationality is ≥25% of the firm's PMETs and other criteria are weak. Korea E-7 renewals can fail if pay falls below the annual floor. Malaysia counts basic salary only. Australia's Core stream takes 4–9 months (published percentiles).
- **R2. No offer, senior pay.** Use the gate you already pass:
  - Taiwan Gold Card (≥NT$160,000 a month; software engineers: check the Digital field).
  - Hong Kong TTPS A (≥HK$2.5m) or B.
  - Singapore ONE Pass (≥S$30,000 fixed from an established employer; AI & Tech from 2027-01-28).
  - Korea Top-Tier (above 3× GNI per capita, listed fields).
  - Japan J-Skip (¥20m plus qualification); it is an HSP status, not a job-seeker permit (D).
  - Failure modes: equity-heavy pay misses Singapore's fixed floor. Taiwan needs a tax statement showing "salary", not payslips alone. Hong Kong extension needs a job or business. Korea's field list may exclude your sector.
- **R3. Graduate within five years.** Options:
  - Hong Kong TTPS C (quota, first-come).
  - Japan J-Find (top-100 university).
  - Taiwan top-200 route to MOL (verify the amendment is in force).
  - Australia MATES (India only; ballot).
  - Failure modes: quota exhaustion, ranking cut-offs and ballot luck.
- **R4. Settlement clocks (documented).**
  - Taiwan: 1 year at ≥NT$6m with a master's, otherwise 3 years.
  - Japan: 1 year at 80 points, otherwise 3 years.
  - Korea Top-Tier: 3 years (vendor).
  - Australia: 186 TRT after 2 years with the same sponsor.
  - New Zealand: Green List Tier 1 is straight to residence.
  - Hong Kong: 7 years.
  - Singapore: discretionary, within 6 months of applying.
  - Malaysia: none found.
  - Failure mode: Japan's guideline and fee changes, and discretionary decisions elsewhere.
- **R5. Timing.** Check these dates before acting:
  - 2026-12-01: DE Rantau reopens on new terms.
  - 2027-01-01: Singapore floors rise.
  - 2027-01-27 and 2027-01-28: Tech.Pass closes and the ONE Pass AI & Tech track opens.
  - By 2027-03-31: Japan HSP ordinance.
  - 2027-04-01: Japan PR guideline tests.
- Re-verify each status on its official page within 14 days of acting (D-007). Judgement; D.

**What would change these rules:**
- Japan publishes the new HSP income figure.
- Taiwan's implementing regulations differ from reports.
- Hong Kong's Talent List adds AI roles.
- INZ opens the TEE category.
- Australia indexes thresholds on 1 July 2027.
- MDEC's reopening terms differ from the notices I read.
