## Research Question

**Decision question.** What should a job seeker be told about the Research Engineer / Member of Technical Staff (MTS) role: what the job is, how much demand and pay it carries, which skills, proof and credentials it requires, how candidates are interviewed, how careers progress and where the jobs are? The answer informs D-002, D-004 and D-015 and the role's scores, skill tiers, titles, role page and PhD-to-industry track.

**Reading of the brief.** "Role" means engineering for frontier-model research: pre-training, post-training and reinforcement learning (RL), evaluation, training data and research infrastructure, whatever the title. A posting counts toward the sample only if I opened it on the employer's own site.

**Method.**
1. I read the Anthropic board (job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic), six of its postings, one xAI posting and the Anthropic Fellows page directly (7 of the 19 coded records).
2. Subagents opened the other employer pages and coded 12 records: a Claude Sonnet 4.6 agent (OpenAI, xAI, Thinking Machines, Microsoft AI, Amazon), a Claude Haiku 5.5 agent for two India postings (Sarvam; Google DeepMind Bangalore) and a Claude Haiku 5.5 agent for two further postings (Google DeepMind Gemini post-training; Mistral). Fields come from page text and were not re-checked line by line by a second reader.
3. Two Claude Sonnet 5.5 evidence agents appended notes and records to files before they were ended (demand, pay and sponsorship; interviews, proof of work, routes, durability and India). Their content is merged here as 32 sources (S14 to S45) and 61 records; their searches stopped where the limit ended each agent, which Absences records.
4. Search snippets from aggregators gave pay cross-checks (grade B, "Extract").
5. Skill shares are computed from the sample's required and preferred text; a skill is counted only if the text names it.
6. Falsification: I tested the knowledge base's PhD, PyTorch and DSA claims against the sample, its LCA position counts and pay figures against aggregated filings and Levels.fyi, and looked for India roles at labs.

Of 25 subagent launches, 2 finished and 23 were ended by usage limits.

**Sampling bias.** Postings were surfaced through search engines and employer boards, which over-represent large English-language US employers. Frontier labs supply 15 of 18 distinct postings; Anthropic alone supplies five, so its template (hybrid policy, visa sentence, "Bachelor's or equivalent" logistics block) colours the degree, years, visa and pay findings. The India postings were found by a dedicated India search, so their share of the sample says nothing about market share. One record is an internship (Mistral), kept as the only entry-level posting. Employer types missing from the sample: enterprises, consultancies, Indian GCCs, and Europe beyond Zurich, Paris and a London duplicate. Skill shares carry roughly ±20 to 25 percentage points at this n.

**Exclusions** (opened or surfaced and not counted): Apple 200566494 and 200591522 (closed); a Meta FAIR text-data role (closed per an aggregator copy); an NVIDIA foundation-model infrastructure role (robotics focus, stale); Amazon 2871450 "Member of Technical Staff (Research Director)" (research leadership, not engineering); Reflection AI "Member of Technical Staff - Evaluations" (aggregator copy "posted 6+ months ago"); three Microsoft Bangalore infrastructure titles (including job 4480853009, a web-heavy efficiency role); Google DeepMind Project Astra (London, closed); Anthropic 5197337008 (redirected to the board: closed); one OpenAI RL/reasoning page and OpenAI's "Research Engineer, San Francisco" page (both returned a client error). Four in-scope live postings were opened and not coded as skill records: xAI 5173208007 (Palo Alto; US$324K-396K) and Amazon 2879015 (SDE II, AGI Foundations post-training; base US$129.3K-223.6K), left out by per-employer caps (figures as reported by the sampling agent; no ledger row), and xAI 4800108007 (Search Post Training) and Thinking Machines 5013924008 (Infrastructure, Inference), which the evidence agent read for loop, degree and pay only.

## Recommendation

1. **If you have about four or more years of production engineering and some ML-training exposure, apply straight to postings that name your layer** (research infrastructure, RL systems, evaluation, data). Evidence: the Interpretability RE asks for 5-10+ years of software experience; RL Distributed Systems lists ML experience only as preferred; Microsoft AI asks for 4+ years [S1, S6]. *Failure mode:* many postings are level-agnostic or evergreen, response rates are unmeasured, and offices are US-centred with in-office expectations (Anthropic: at least 25% of the time).
2. **If you lack that profile, use a fellowship or residency as the entry route.** Anthropic Fellows requires fluent Python and work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada, but no prior experience [S2, S39]. *Failure mode:* four full-time months, no visa sponsorship, no offer guarantee, an offer rate that is an employer claim, and listings with different cohort dates (check the start date before applying).
3. **Build proof as artefacts showing scale and rigour** (an eval suite, a debugged training run, a reproduced result), and keep a short "most proud of" write-up ready: xAI and Anthropic forms ask for one [S1, S4]. For infrastructure roles add open-source work in the serving or training stack, which Thinking Machines names as preferred [S5, S37]. *Failure mode:* what reviewers weigh most was not measured here.
4. **If you are in India, target the few India-located roles and remote-friendly lab postings, and plan for visas.** Google DeepMind Bangalore and Sarvam were live on 2026-11-13 [S8, S9]; Anthropic's Bengaluru office is a leading indicator and not a posting [S44]. *Failure mode:* two India postings in 18, no posted INR pay, and lab sponsorship is "not for every role".
5. **Prepare for coding and applied-LLM interviews even for "research" titles** (Python-only loops, coding assessments, ML fundamentals) and ask each employer for its AI-tool policy in writing: Anthropic bars AI in take-homes and live interviews unless the assessment says otherwise, OpenAI's guide is silent, and Meta was reported to be piloting AI-assisted coding rounds [S1, S4, S29, S30, S34, S35]. *Failure mode:* the loops come from two employer descriptions, one guide and five anonymous reports.
6. **Tier skills as: Python and one deep-learning framework (Must); evals, post-training/RL, distributed training, collaboration (Important); treat data structures and algorithms as an interview skill and not a posting requirement.** *Failure mode:* n = 18.
7. **If you need sponsorship, treat it as a per-role question and ask at the first screen.** Wage level affects H-1B selection from the FY2027 registration season and the US$100,000 payment is litigated [S19, S20, S21, S23]. *Failure mode:* the rule summaries stop at 2026-09 and this is not legal advice.

*What would change these rules:* Levels.fyi pages for the exact titles and official LCA files that move pay; a 30+ posting sample with more of Europe, startups and enterprises; more candidate loop reports; India pay data; the status of the H-1B payment after 2026-09.

## Detailed Findings

### D1. Definition, variants and aliases

The role is the engineering half of frontier research. Examples in the sample: scaling "distributed training jobs to thousands of GPUs" and "optimizer development" (Anthropic pre-training); reinforcement-learning environments and evals (OpenAI Frontier Evals & Environments); petabyte-scale pre-training data (xAI); a failure-tolerant RL distributed system (Anthropic RL Distributed Systems, which asks for distributed-systems depth and lists ML only as preferred); post-training recipes and model behaviour (Microsoft AI, Anthropic Zurich, Google DeepMind Gemini); multimodal and agent research (Mistral, Sarvam). Boundary with adjacent roles: an infrastructure-heavy posting can carry a Research Engineer title (A04), and a training-performance role can carry none (OpenAI "Training Performance Engineer", counted as adjacent).

## Open Questions & Risks

**Strongest objections an expert would raise.**
1. *n = 18 and five of them Anthropic.* Supported: skill shares are ±20 to 25 points and Anthropic's template drives degree, visa and pay findings. Against: findings are stated as sample facts with n, and conclusions lean on employer wording, not shares.
2. *Postings are not hiring bars.* "Bachelor's or equivalent" is boilerplate, and hires may be PhD-heavy all the same. Supported: no hire-background source was collected. This limits K12 to "not a stated minimum".
