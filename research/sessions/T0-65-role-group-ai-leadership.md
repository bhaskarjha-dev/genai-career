S-numbers refer to rows in the Sources & Evidence Ledger. Grades follow EGP-1.0 (A primary, B platform or established secondary, C attributed third party, D memory or judgement, E unverifiable, M own calculation, P practice-curated). ## Research Question

**Decision question.** What must the knowledge base know about `ai-engineering-manager`, `vp-head-of-ai` and `chief-ai-officer` to describe the job, demand, pay, skills, proof, credentials, interviews, careers and locations accurately, and which of K1 to K25 should change?

**Sub-questions.** (1) What do postings for each role ask for, and which aliases do they use? (2) How much demand and which pay bands can be measured, with dates? (3) Which skill tiers in the role cards do the postings support? (4) What do interview loops, entry routes and progression look like? (5) Where are the jobs, and what is different in India? (6) What do government mandates and executive-search sources add for the CAIO?

**Reading of the brief.** I read "AI Engineering Manager" as someone who leads engineers building, serving or evaluating LLM or ML systems; generic engineering-manager roles at AI companies are excluded. "Head of AI" covers a function owner, with the hands-on and strategy variants kept apart. CAIO means an enterprise or public-sector executive titled Chief AI Officer or an equivalent combined title.

**Method.**
- **Search and reads.** I ran about 25 searches (standard and extended modes) on title strings across Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS and Workable domains, company careers pages, and aggregators (Fast AI Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor, Dice, ZipRecruiter). I read nine pages as text: the OMB memo, the Anthropic and Accela postings on employer boards, a SandboxAQ aggregator copy, IBM's press release, a CFO Dive article, the Fast AI Jobs EM page, Axial Search's CAIO listing and a third-party-hosted copy of SBI's advertisement. Fetches of a Novo Nordisk page and of NVIDIA's careers page returned no job text. The fetch tool opens only URLs that appeared as search-result links, so most Ashby and Greenhouse pages could not be opened.
- **Counting rule.** A posting counts if it shows a date on or after 2026-08-13, or if I opened it live. Undated or older postings are kept as flagged, supplementary records for wording and pay patterns only.
- **Biases.** Search indexes over-represent large employers, English text and US postings. The Fast AI Jobs index covers AI startups only. Recruiter copies duplicate employer postings. I capped Anthropic and NVIDIA at three postings each. LinkedIn was not accessed.
- **Exclusions.**
  - 38 of the 50 EM rows on the Fast AI Jobs page were non-AI (infrastructure, security, GTM, TPM or assistant roles).
  - 7 of 15 Anthropic EM titles were non-AI-system teams (brand web, security, GRC, UI, growth, enterprise product and data engineering for product).
  - About 14 EM or Director postings dated before 2026-08-13 were not counted. They include consulting "Manager" grades at Deloitte, EY and GlobalLogic, and postings at EdCast, Citi, Precisely, BILT, Ottimate and Front.
  - Search results for "Chief of Staff", "Chief Business Officer – AI Services" and "Founding CEO" were dropped as not CAIO.
- **Falsification searches.**
  - Independent counts of named CAIOs against IBM's 76%.
  - Evidence of management-layer thinning against EM demand growth.
  - Whether Head of AI is an executive role or a hands-on one.
  - Whether aggregator EM counts are AI-specific.
  - Levels.fyi against an unsourced Anthropic EM compensation claim.

## Recommendation

1. **EM.** If you have 6 to 8 or more years of engineering and at least 2 years leading people (a tech lead counts at some employers), target teams that build or serve LLM systems. Filter by team charter, not by "AI" in the title.
   - Evidence that wins: platform, eval and devtools delivery adopted across teams, and a hiring record.
   - Failure mode: lab EM bars ask for 3+ years of management, and aggregator counts overstate AI-specific roles by about four to five times.
2. **Head of AI.** Classify the posting first.
   - Player-coach (reports to CEO or CTO, ships production LLM systems): evidence is shipped systems, evals and first hires; the US base anchor is $240K to $315K.
   - Strategy or enablement lead: evidence is cross-functional governance, portfolio and ROI. Accela's posting also expects prototype-level LLM, retrieval, agent and evaluation skills, and lists governance credentials as examples. Anchors are $170K to $290K (VP) and $240K to $280K.
   - Ask about reporting line, team size and budget. Failure mode: title inflation, where you are the first AI hire with no team or budget.
3. **CAIO.** Do not plan around open postings. Routes are internal appointment, public-sector or higher-education postings (US $117K to $200K; SBI up to ₹160 lakh), and retained search. Build governance artefacts and board exposure.
   - Failure modes: dual-hatted roles, tenure of about two years (Avasant, C), and a federal mandate that designates existing officials.
4. **India.** Target GCCs, product firms and public-sector notifications. Expect 10 to 15+ years for director-level titles, and do not read a Big-4 "Manager" title as people management.
5. **Maintainers (D-002, D-004, D-015).**
   - Add the aliases found (K2, K12, K22).
   - Rewrite K23 as a survey figure alongside counted prevalence.
   - For EM, move technical leadership and hiring up, and downgrade GRC, guardrails, capacity cost and observability.
   - For Head of AI, add hands-on production AI as a variant-specific Must, lower the pay score's centre and widen its range.
   - Keep demand and durability scores, adding a management-thinning risk note.

**What would change this.** 15 or more dated CAIO postings on employer boards would move CAIO demand. Ten or more Levels.fyi submissions for lab EMs would move the pay evidence. LCA counts would move the sponsorship notes. A Head of AI sample of 30 or more would move the variant split.

### 2. AI Engineering Manager / Director of AI Engineering

**Definition and variants.** The role leads engineers who build, serve or evaluate LLM or ML systems.
- **Frontier-lab platform and eval EMs.** Anthropic's Agent Prompts & Evals post leads a platform team whose customers are other engineers. It wants platform, infrastructure or devtools management experience and says no research background is needed (A, COI).
- **Applied-AI and forward-deployed managers.** Examples are Anthropic's Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering (London, Paris), OpenAI's Applied AI Engineering Manager (Startups), Google's Forward Deployed Engineering Manager, GenAI (Bengaluru; dated 2026-03-20, outside the window) and a London listing for a forward-deployed EM leading 8+ engineers on Databricks-based data and AI solutions (employer not named in the extract) (B).
- **AI-product EMs** at scale-ups (Harvey agents, Plata AI platform, Retool Core AI) (B).
- **GPU and inference infrastructure EMs** at NVIDIA (B).
- **Consulting "Manager" grade in India.** Deloitte req 101515 lists the designation Manager with the role Generative AI Engineer (B).
- **Boundaries.** An AI Engineer or staff IC is hands-on; a Head of AI owns the whole function. "Lead AI Engineer" and "AI Engineering Lead" postings are often IC-lead roles and were excluded.

Aliases in the counted sample: Engineering Manager, [AI team] (8); Manager, [AI or Data] Engineering (2); Applied AI … Manager (2); Director of Engineering, Core AI (1); Senior Software Engineering Manager, Generative AI (1); Machine Learning Engineering Manager (1). Supplementary: Sr Manager – ML & AI (HERE); Director, Applied AI Engineering – Enterprise (Scale AI); Director–AI Engineering Solutions (Novo Nordisk GBS).

### 4. Chief AI Officer

**Definition and variants.** The executive owns an organisation's AI strategy, adoption and governance. Variants seen:
- **Public-sector principal officer.** Illinois DoIT's CAIO is the agency's principal executive and policy-making official for AI and ML, reporting to the Deputy Secretary (B; outside the window).
- **Higher-education seats.** The College of Charleston's "Director, Chief AI Officer" reports to the CIO. The UNC System's CAIO sits in the office of the Chief Operating Officer, which reports to the President (B).
- **Bank officer.** SBI's GM & Chief AI Officer is a fixed-term executive who leads AI implementation, platform delivery and governance, including oversight of an independent model-validation team (B; notice read in full).
- **Search-firm template.** Axial's evergreen listing (role closed) describes a CAIO reporting to the CEO or CTO, with 12+ years and 5+ at director, VP or C level, leading 10 to 50+ people, owning vendor and build-versus-buy choices and speaking to boards (C, COI).
- **Combined-title and dual-hatted roles.** Examples are Chief Technology & AI Officer and the CPSC's CAIO, who stays chief data officer (C).

Aliases: Chief Data/AI Officer; Chief AI & Innovation Officer; Chief Health AI Officer; GM & Chief AI Officer; Director, Chief AI Officer.

**Government mandate.** OMB M-25-21 is dated 2025-04-03; the designation duty runs 60 days from issue (see the `rule` record). It applies to agency CAIOs, not to a "VP or Head of AI". In India, the AI-governance post in SBI's notice refers to RBI directions and the DPDP Act (B).

## Open Questions & Risks

**Strongest objections.**
1. **Sample bias and size.** Skill shares at n=7 to 11 are weak, and most pages were extracts. This is correct, and the brief's own tolerance is about ±18 points at n=30. The shares are used only as a cross-check. The gap between a 30.9% platform share for GRC and 0/8 in the EM sample is large enough to flag.
2. **IBM's 76% is the primary figure.** It is, as published by IBM, but it is a CEO survey of 2,000 respondents and not a count. Crist Kolder included "equivalent" titles (10.7%) and ProfessionLens included combined titles (10.8%). Survey frames probably skew to larger organisations.
3. **Base pay understates lab compensation.** Posted bands omit equity, and Levels.fyi has few Anthropic EM submissions. The unsourced $500K to $900K+ claim cannot be checked, so I report no total-compensation range for lab EMs.
4. **The AI share of EM rows is title-based.** Many "Engineering Manager" roles at AI startups lead AI products. The 20 to 24% figure is an M estimate from 50 listed rows.
5. **CAIO volume sits in confidential searches.** True. The appointment and census evidence addresses this partly, but unadvertised hiring is not measured.
6. **Undated Head of AI postings may be stale.** 8 of 15 are undated and flagged, and pay measures use dated items first.
7. **The 15-postings-per-role standard was not met with full reads.** Correct; two employer-board pages and one copy of an employer notice were read in full, and the rest are extracts.

**Risks.**
- Fetch was blocked or empty on Ashby, Workday, Novo Nordisk and NVIDIA careers pages.
- The NVIDIA requisition JR2020646 appears under two titles and dates in captured copies; the 2026-08-28 LLM Inference capture was counted.
- The Anthropic postings are COI.
- Vendor pay studies are COI.
- Axial's page is an evergreen talent-network listing, so its pay band and requirements describe a template, not an open requisition.

## Discovered Concerns

- Several pages repeat IBM's 76% as if it were a count (for example Second Talent and TechJack). Any KB page doing this has a circularity risk.
- The Rework 43% Fortune 500 CAIO figure and its "94 LinkedIn appointments" claim could not be traced to LinkedIn data (E).
- Glassdoor India's ₹3L to ₹10L estimates for director roles are implausible. Do not import them.
- Fast AI Jobs' EM page mixes in TPM and assistant rows, so direct use inflates AI EM counts.
- Some interview-guide and salary sites publish AI-written pages with undisclosed methods.
- Search-firm "job" pages can be evergreen listings (Axial's CAIO page states that its role is closed and that it collects candidates for future roles). Counting them as live postings would overstate CAIO demand.
