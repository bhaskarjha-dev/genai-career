## Research Question

**Decision question.** What must the knowledge base tell a job seeker about fractional AI leadership, independent AI consulting and domain evaluation work: what the job is, demand, pay, skills, proof, credentials, interviews, careers and where jobs are?

**Scope applied.** Three role IDs only. Postings served as open on 2026-11-13 or 2026-11-14, or dated on or after 2026-08-13. Other evidence from 2025-01-01; older items are flagged.

**Method.**
- 26 standard-mode searches and 25 page fetches (4 failed or returned only a title).
- Queries covered fractional CAIO and Head of AI jobs; Go Fractional and Fractionus listings; Mercor, Scale AI and Outlier, micro1, Turing, Alignerr, Invisible, Handshake AI and xAI expert postings; US 1099 and UK day-rate AI contracts; India AI consultant and evaluation roles; UK contract rates (IT Jobs Watch); LinkedIn's 2026 Jobs on the Rise; marketplace fees; contract templates; the India Copyright Act.
- Opened in full: 6 fractional pages, 3 Mercor listings, 3 Scale AI and Outlier pages, 1 Turing page, 5 UK contract pages, 2 IT Jobs Watch data pages and Mercor's own site index.
- Sampling bias. The search index is US-only and English-language. Domain filters returned off-domain results, so no Lever or Ashby postings surfaced, and Greenhouse appeared only for Scale AI, Alignerr and Invisible. The UK contract postings come from one aggregator's live listing; Mercor supplies 4 of 18 evaluation postings; the fractional sample leans on one UK advertiser (4) and one executive-search firm (4).
- Falsification searches. I fetched the live IT Jobs Watch page to test the claimed UK rate fall (it held). I looked for contract roles among India "AI consultant" results (2 of 17 found). I searched for the source of the "92% find clients through networks" claim (not found; other surveys give 53–75%).

## Recommendation

1. **Fractional leader.** Treat this as a senior, network-sourced route, not a job-board route.
   - Qualify first: every posting that states experience asks for 10+ years or 5+ years as a fractional executive, and most want a prior head, VP or CTO seat. Go Fractional's membership page asks for 7+ years of experience and 5+ in leadership or fractional ownership [S31].
   - Apply to curated networks, executive-search benches and fractional marketplaces, and keep your own pipeline. Expect to be placed when a client appears; treat a posting as a bench entry, not a vacancy.
   - Price at $150–250 an hour as an ask. Expect $75–150 on employer-side postings and from $5,000 a month on managed marketplaces.
   - Show 2+ shipped AI projects, each with one business metric, plus case studies. A CAIO certificate is not a requirement (1 of 13, nice-to-have).
   - *Failure modes:* bench pages with no live engagement; templated pages whose posted rates contradict their own copy; vertical gates (Estrin requires a law-firm background).
2. **Independent consultant or contractor.**
   - Use agency contract postings as a floor check: $50–80 an hour in the US sample and £500–1,000 a day in the UK sample.
   - In the UK, the median has held at £550 for two years while ad volume tripled. Co-occurring skills: Python 34.6%, LLM 34.1%, Azure 29.7%, ML 28.5%, stakeholder management 27.4%, RAG 23.7%, AWS 23.2% [S1].
   - In India, "AI consultant" titles are employee roles; for independent work plan on export clients and put duration and territory into every IP assignment.
   - *Failure modes:* rate compression; 1099 versus W2 ambiguity; UK postings that need clearance or UK residence; Toptal-style non-circumvention terms.
3. **Evaluation specialist.** Treat expert-grader contracts as supplementary or bridge income; treat in-house evaluation engineering (India GCCs and IT services) as the salaried route.
   - Join each relevant expert pool once with a domain credential and years in function. Expect waits from a week to months, project-level selection, early endings and visa limits.
   - Pay: $20–70 an hour for generalist and entry work, $60–120 for professional fields, $130–300 for senior legal, medical and finance experts. Lead with the credential, not an "AI trainer" label.
   - *Failure modes:* no placement (referral-site stats of 6% and 2%, D); misclassification disputes (one unverified report, D); unstable pay averages; no H-1B or STEM OPT support at Mercor.
4. **What would change these rules.** Employer-board fractional postings in volume (none seen); Go Fractional or Fractional Jobs counts; India rates for any of the three roles; in-house evaluation-lead loops; a full 15-posting sample per role with posting dates.

## Open Questions & Risks

- **Strongest objections.**
  - A snippet-heavy, aggregator-heavy sample is not a market sample. For: 32 of 48 postings are copies and many lack dates. Against: the UK platform data (n=1,466) and the Scale, Turing and Mercor pages opened in full show the same pattern.
  - Mercor ranges are not earnings. For: Bloomberg's relay says Mercor lacks the share of tasks at its $110 average. Against: listing ranges and the $95–110 averages are broadly consistent.
  - Fractional work is network-sourced, so few postings does not mean low demand. For: K8 is untraced. Against: nothing found.
  - UK GenAI skill-citing ads are not consultant roles. For: they count skill mentions. I used them only for rates and co-occurrence.
  - Fee data comes from competitors. For: every source is a rival or comparison site. Against: independent sites agree on 0% talent fees for Toptal, Contra and Braintrust and on 0–15% for Upwork (one says a flat 10%).
  - Template effects. Four Axial pages and four pages from one UK advertiser may overstate shared skills; dropping the Axial pages moves shares by up to 12 points.
- **Risks.**
  - Fractional sample 2 short of target; US- and UK-weighted.
  - 9 of 13 fractional pages show no posting date; the UK advertiser's pages may predate the window.
  - Mercor's misclassification report comes from a review site and is unverified against court records.
  - OneSeven's posting is dated 2026-03-10 but was served as open on 2026-11-13.
  - Evaluation shares mix two variants (expert graders and evaluation engineers).

## Discovered Concerns

- Mercor's listings are standing pools, so any demand measure built on listing counts overstates vacancies [S4].
- Mercor's headline pay average moved from $141 to $80 on a relabel, so cite averages with source and date [S11].
- Axial's pages say they are not specific roles and post rates that contradict their own copy [S24].
- Mercor does not support H-1B or STEM OPT candidates, and Handshake does not support STEM OPT [S4][S47].
- UK contract postings often carry inside-IR35 terms, clearance or UK-residence conditions [S66][S68].
- The evaluation role card mixes two variants. Entry routes and pay differ: expert-grader contracts are remote and hourly; evaluation-engineering roles in India are salaried, office-based and Python-heavy.
